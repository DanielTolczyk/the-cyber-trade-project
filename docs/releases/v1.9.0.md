### Executive Summary

Release **v1.9.0** marks the complete ratification of the entire 9-discipline Specialty Endorsement suite within The Cybersecurity Trade Project. Moving beyond preliminary drafts and placeholders, this minor release establishes concrete, vendor-neutral, mathematically grounded Three-Layer architectural building codes across every major discipline in the cybersecurity engineering trade.

In addition to ratifying dedicated 2,000-hour post-licensure runtime specifications for all 9 tracks, v1.9.0 introduces the comprehensive **Master Cross-Crediting & Articulation Matrix**, streamlines framework-wide technical governance terminology, eliminates redundant parenthetical metaphors across formal specifications, and expands the documentation portal with full sidebar navigation across all specialty tracks.

### Key Architectural & Specification Ratifications

#### 1. Ratification of All 9 Specialty Endorsement Specifications
Every specialty discipline is now governed by an authoritative Three-Layer normative standard (NIST NICE Work Roles, Consensus Technical Standards, and Operational Runtime Ledgers with Master of Record Authority):

* **`SE-MED` (Medical Device & Clinical Technology Security):** Life-safety engineering covering SiMD, SaMD, mobile apps, and clinical cloud systems, anchored to FDA Section 524B, AAMI TIR57, ANSI/AAMI/ISO 14971, and IEC 62304 (+20% to +25% statutory wage adder).
* **`SE-ICS` (Industrial Control Systems & Operational Technology Security):** Critical infrastructure protection across Levels 0-3 and the Level 3.5 IDMZ, anchored to ISA/IEC 62443, NIST SP 800-82r3, NERC CIP, and IEC 61511 Safety Instrumented Systems (SIS), enforcing a mandatory 1:1 supervisory ratio during live plant maintenance (+20% to +25% statutory wage adder).
* **`SE-DFIR` (Digital Forensics & Incident Response):** High-liability evidentiary triage, malware reverse engineering, and threat containment, anchored to ISO/IEC 27037, ISO/IEC 27042, NIST SP 800-86, RFC 3227, and Federal Rules of Evidence FRE 902(13)/(14) (+15% to +20% statutory wage adder).
* **`SE-CLD` (Cloud Security & Infrastructure Architecture):** Multi-cloud identity federation, Kubernetes hardening, CIEM/CSPM, and policy-as-code deployment pipelines, anchored to CSA CCM v4, CIS Cloud Benchmarks, NIST SP 800-145, and FedRAMP Rev 5 (+10% to +15% architectural wage adder).
* **`SE-OFF` (Offensive Security & Adversary Emulation):** Controlled adversary simulation, exploit chaining, and active detection validation, anchored to MITRE ATT&CK, PTES, NIST SP 800-115, and OSSTMM v3 (+15% to +20% high-risk operational wage adder).
* **`SE-AIML` (AI & Machine Learning Security Assurance):** Adversarial ML threat modeling, prompt injection defense, model weights provenance, and LLM guardrails, anchored to NIST AI RMF (AI 100-1), OWASP Top 10 for LLMs, MITRE ATLAS, and ISO/IEC 42001 (+15% to +20% emerging technology wage adder).
* **`SE-APP` (Application & Software Product Security):** Secure SDLC, threat modeling, automated CI/CD security gating, and SBOM lifecycle management, anchored to OWASP SAMM v2.0, OWASP ASVS v4.0, NIST SSDF (SP 800-218), and SLSA v1.0 (Tier 1 Baseline).
* **`SE-ICAM` (Identity, Credential & Access Management):** Enterprise federation (SAML/OIDC), Privileged Access Management (PAM), passwordless FIDO2/WebAuthn, and Zero Trust identity fabric, anchored to NIST SP 800-63-3/4 and CISA Zero Trust baselines (Tier 1 Baseline).
* **`SE-PKI` (Cryptographic Infrastructure & PKI):** Multi-tier CA hierarchies, Hardware Security Modules (HSMs), key ceremonies, and Post-Quantum Cryptography migration, anchored to NIST SP 800-57, FIPS 140-3, and NIST PQC FIPS 203/204/205 (Tier 1 Baseline).


#### 2. Master Cross-Crediting & Articulation Matrix
Codifies strict, asymmetric, and mathematically capped cross-crediting rules across all 9 disciplines to prevent credential dilution:
* **Evidentiary Isolation (`SE-DFIR`):** Forensic chain of custody and incident response require 100% domain-specific runtime (0 hours cross-credit from general software development).
* **Physical Process Isolation (`SE-ICS`):** Commercial IT and web application security provide 0 hours of cross-credit toward plant control systems. Up to 500 hours recognized articulation is permitted only for licensed industrial electricians, instrumentation technicians, or Navy nuclear propulsion operators.
* **Asymmetric Articulation (`SE-MED` vs `SE-APP`):** `SE-MED` serves as a superset of software product security; `SE-APP` practitioners receive up to 1,000 hours credit toward `SE-MED` for shared foundations and must complete 1,000 mandatory clinical and FDA regulatory hours on live medical technology.
