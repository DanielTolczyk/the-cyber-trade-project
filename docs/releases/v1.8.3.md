# The Cybersecurity Trade Project - Release Notes v1.8.3

**Release Title:** Expand Geographic Districts and Add Island Territory Protections  
**Release Date:** September 1, 2026  
**SemVer Classification:** Patch Release (Normative Governance & Specification Refinement)  
**Distribution Repositories:** `framework`, `estimator`, `logbook`, `clearinghouse` under `the-cyber-trade-project`

---

### Executive Summary

Release **v1.8.3** expands the normative geographic governance of The Cybersecurity Trade Project to comprehensively cover all 50 U.S. States, the District of Columbia, and all five inhabited U.S. Territories (Puerto Rico, U.S. Virgin Islands, Guam, American Samoa, and Northern Mariana Islands). This release introduces the Non-Contiguous States and Island Territories Standard, codifies anti-isolation living wage protections against remote maritime logistics costs, establishes Pacific defense enclave wage floors, harmonizes rotational domain tokens across ecosystem tooling, aligns clearinghouse prior learning assessment (PLA) calculation caps with the 50% residency floor, and bridges end-to-end logbook-to-clearinghouse submission bundle export interoperability.

---

### Key Architectural & Governance Ratifications

#### 1. Comprehensive Territorial Geographic Allocation
* **District 1 (Northeast & Mid-Atlantic Hub):** Formally incorporates Puerto Rico (PR) and the U.S. Virgin Islands (USVI) alongside the Mid-Atlantic and New England maritime corridor.
* **District 3 (Western & Pacific Rim Hub):** Formally incorporates Alaska (AK), Hawaii (HI), Guam (GU), American Samoa (AS), and the Northern Mariana Islands (MP) into the unified Pacific theater.

#### 2. Non-Contiguous States & Island Territories Standard
* **Anti-Isolation Wage Floor:** Barring sponsoring employers from classifying practitioners in non-contiguous states or island territories under the Zone 3 Rural floor due to elevated logistics, energy, and supply chain expenses.
* **Defense & Critical Enclave Alignment:** Pacific theater operational nodes (Guam, Hawaii, Alaska) supporting INDOPACOM, PACAF, missile defense, and trans-Pacific submarine cable landing stations default to Zone 1 (Metro Core / High COLI) prevailing baselines to prevent talent attrition.
* **Autonomous Island Charters:** Authorizing any territory with 50+ active Journeymen (e.g., Local 109 San Juan or Local 312 Honolulu) to charter dedicated JATC community college trusts.

#### 3. Domain Token Harmonization & Mathematical Parity
* **Domain Enum Harmonization:** Standardized canonical domain tokens (`D1_PERIMETER_CLOUD`, `D2_DETECTION_SOC`, `D3_IDENTITY_IAM`, `D4_VULN_ATTACK`, `D5_DEFENSIVE_GRC`) while maintaining backward-compatible dual-token alias resolution across digital logbook ledgers.
* **PLA Cap Synchronization:** Aligned the Clearinghouse statutory evaluator to enforce the canonical 4,000-hour Prior Learning Assessment (PLA) maximum cap (50% Residency Floor).
* **Master Practitioner Elevation Evaluator:** Extended the Clearinghouse wage evaluation engine to evaluate 12,000+ verified operational runtime hours for Master Practitioner tier standing.

#### 4. End-to-End Logbook-to-Clearinghouse Interoperability Bridge
* Integrated one-click JATC Submission Bundle generation into the Universal Digital Logbook PWA, automatically calculating Merkle tree roots for instant drag-and-drop validation in the Clearinghouse portal.
