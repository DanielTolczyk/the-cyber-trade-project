#### 3. International Practitioner Portability, Foreign Credential Evaluation & Visa Equity (`governance/transition-plan.md` & `FAQ.md`)
* **Section 3.C (Transition Plan):** Codified direct lateral challenge pathways (Track A / Track C) for experienced international engineers with 8,000+ verified operational hours, allowing foreign-trained practitioners to earn Journeyman Licensure without repeating entry-level apprenticeships.
* **Prevailing Wage (RJPB) Protections:** Mandated that participating employers sponsoring H-1B, TN, O-1, or L-1 visa workers compensate at or above 100% of the Regional Journeyman Prevailing Benchmark under 20 CFR § 655.731, eliminating wage suppression and consulting body-shop undercutting.
* **Immigration Sponsorship Anti-Coercion Shield:** Classified threats to revoke or delay visa sponsorships as a Class C Major Ethical Violation, supported by confidential Ombudsperson representation under the Blind Whistleblower Proxy.
* **FAQ Suite Expansion:** Added **FAQ 46** providing practical guidance on foreign credential evaluation, international career mobility, and cleared defense statutory boundaries.
