### Executive Summary

Release **v1.9.2** codifies the turnkey legal, statutory, and institutional registration assets required for nationwide workforce deployment and multi-employer collective defense.

This release introduces the complete **National Guidelines for Apprenticeship Standards (29 CFR Part 29 & Part 30)** package, ratifies standardized **Taft-Hartley Section 302(c) Training Trust and Employer Participation Agreements (Forms FORM-006 & FORM-007)**, publishes the **Model State Cybersecurity Trade Licensing Act & Interstate Practice Compact (ICPC)**, and unifies navigation across the newly released **Insurance Telemetry Gateway (`cyber-trade-telemetry`)**.

---

### Key Architectural & Governance Ratifications

#### 1. Department of Labor (DOL) National Apprenticeship Guidelines (29 CFR Part 29 & Part 30)
* **Appendix A (8,000-Hour Work Process Schedule):** Turnkey operational work process breakdown across the 5 mandatory domains (Domain 1: 1,500 hrs, Domain 2: 2,000 hrs, Domain 3: 1,500 hrs, Domain 4: 1,500 hrs, Domain 5: 1,500 hrs) with 20 concrete NIST SP 800-181 task codes and supervisory sign-offs.
* **Appendix B (576-Hour Related Technical Instruction Syllabus):** Turnkey 4-year curriculum (144 hrs/year) for public community colleges and accredited Certified Trade Instructors (CTI).
* **Appendix C (Equal Opportunity & Civil Rights):** Objective rubric-based candidate selection, veteran credit crosswalks, neurodivergent accessibility accommodations, and anti-harassment protections.

#### 2. Turnkey Regional JATC Legal Instruments
* **Form FORM-006 (JATC Multi-Employer Trust Agreement):** Standard Agreement and Declaration of Trust under Taft-Hartley Section 302(c) and ERISA establishing bipartite parity governance to fund community college lab environments, subsidize CTI instructors, and guarantee $0-tuition instruction for registered apprentices.
* **Form FORM-007 (Employer Participation Agreement):** Standard binding contract for Participating Employer Council (PEC) members committing to mandatory 2:1 on-shift supervisory ratios, hourly training trust remittances, and the 4-tier wage step schedule.

#### 3. Model State Legislation & Interstate Practice Compact (ICPC)
* **Model State Cybersecurity Trade Licensing Act:** Turnkey statutory text for state legislative sponsors creating State Trade Licensing Boards, codifying Journeyman prerequisites, protecting the Right of Technical Refusal (Pillar V), and establishing executive override liability transfers (Form FORM-002).
* **Interstate Cybersecurity Practice Compact (ICPC):** Reciprocity model granting Compact Privileges to Practice across member states and facilitating rapid crisis surge deployments for incident response (DFIR) teams.

#### 4. Ecosystem Navigation Parity & Insurance Telemetry Gateway
* Unified the 5-tool ecosystem header dropdown across all web surfaces (`framework`, `estimator`, `logbook`, `clearinghouse`, `telemetry`).
* Documented the Insurance Telemetry Gateway (`cyber-trade-telemetry`) providing zero-knowledge ratio verification feeds for cyber liability underwriters (CUAAC).
