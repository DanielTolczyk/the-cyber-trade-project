### Key Architectural & Governance Ratifications

#### 1. Systemic Edge Cases & Institutional Defenses Specification (`docs/systemic-edge-cases-and-defense.md`)
* Published an authoritative, 6-domain failure mode analysis documenting institutional threat models and mitigations:
  * **Domain 1 (Cryptographic Integrity & CI/CD Limits):** Semantic correctness vs. SLSA Level 4 provenance, off-hours MTTR dual-key break-glass bypasses, compensating control equivalence and Rice's Theorem, hardware key custody/taint analysis, multi-engine pipeline diversity, and the Building Code Invariant.
  * **Domain 2 (Privacy & Cryptographic Market Security):** Poisson-distributed decoy telemetry (RFC 4303), salted zero-knowledge Pedersen commitments / MNPI firewalls, and civil discovery (FRCP 34) due-care proof.
  * **Domain 3 (Labor Law, Classification & Dispute Jurisdiction):** NLRA Section 2(11) supervisory classification case law (*Oakwood Healthcare*), FLSA computer employee exemptions and standby stipends, LMRA Section 301 restructuring invariants, Federal Arbitration Act (FAA) regulatory standing (*EEOC v. Waffle House*), essential continuity no-strike FOIA arbitration, and Right-to-Work *CWA v. Beck* defense fund firewalls.
  * **Domain 4 (Macroeconomics, Trust Portability & Capital Markets):** Star Market compensation floors vs. SaaS gross margins, ERISA Taft-Hartley Variable Pension (VAPP) stabilization reserves and fixed hourly remittances, Day-Zero community college educational liquidity, Chapter 11 bankruptcy DIP financing invariants, and actuarial loss-frequency vs. tiered cloud sub-limits.
  * **Domain 5 (Workforce Pipeline Integrity & Anti-Gaming Guardrails):** Pre-apprenticeship filtering, universal 8,000-hour generalist core foundation, specialty badging with 300-hour articulation bridges, rotational domain caps, and four-tier graduated employer sanctions.
  * **Domain 6 (Regulatory Attachment, Securities Law & Executive Accountability):** SEC Form 8-K / CIRCIA incident clocks vs. Reg S-K 106 & Rule 10b-5 scienter deterrence, 50-state NAIC regulation and E&S market Freedom of Rate, judicial clearance jurisprudence (*Department of the Navy v. Egan*), first-party vs. third-party software supply chain (TPRM) boundaries, offshore Global Capability Centers (GCCs), and rebuttable retaliation presumptions with 48-hour expedited review panels.

#### 2. Secure In-Memory Client-Side Search Engine (`⌘K` / `Ctrl+K`)
* Integrated a zero-server, static client-side search engine across the web portal.
* **Security Invariants:** 100% in-memory execution over pre-compiled `assets/js/search-index.json`, strict DOM injection prevention (zero `innerHTML` on queries/results), input length caps (max 64 characters), and zero-knowledge privacy with no external network telemetry.

#### 3. Primary FAQ Suite Expansion (`FAQ.md`)
* Added FAQs 39 through 45 answering practical stakeholder questions regarding senior compensation floors, apprentice productivity filtering, NLRA supervisory classification, 50-state E&S insurance mechanics, offshore GCC routing, Day-Zero instructor funding, and apprentice churn protections.

#### 4. Specification Quality Gate Diction & Vocabulary Upgrades (`scripts/validate-specifications.py`)
* Expanded the automated quality gate vocabulary filter to reject corporate marketing jargon and consultant filler (such as pitch-deck buzzwords and vague qualifiers), ensuring 100% concrete statutory and engineering prose.
