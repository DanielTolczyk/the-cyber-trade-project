# The Cybersecurity Trade Project - Release Notes v1.9.1

**Release Title:** Mandatory Mentorship Runtime Standard & Certified Trade Instructor Credential  
**Release Date:** September 1, 2026  
**SemVer Classification:** Patch Release (Normative Licensure & Pedagogical Expansion)  
**Distribution Repositories:** `framework`, `estimator`, `logbook`, `clearinghouse` under `the-cyber-trade-project`

---

### Executive Summary

Release **v1.9.1** establishes the formal pedagogical and mentorship standards required for Master Practitioner elevation. Moving beyond raw operational runtime hours, this release anchors Master standing to the proven transfer of tradecraft to the next generation.

This patch release introduces the **500-hour verified instructional runtime prerequisite for Master elevation**, ratifies the **Certified Trade Instructor (CTI)** endorsement curriculum, and updates the ecosystem tooling (`logbook` and `estimator`) to track instructional mentorship hours.

---

### Key Architectural & Governance Ratifications

#### 1. Mandatory Mentorship Runtime for Master Elevation
* Out of the 4,000 post-Journeyman hours required to attain Master Practitioner standing (12,000 total hours), candidates must log a minimum of **500 verified instructional runtime hours (12.5%)** under Modality A or B, providing direct line-of-sight oversight to registered apprentices.
* Ensures that Master Practitioners demonstrate pedagogical competency and trade leadership before attaining stamping and supervisory authority.

#### 2. Certified Trade Instructor (CTI) Endorsement
* Establishes a 144-hour accredited vocational teaching credential covering:
  - *Module CTI-101:* Adult Vocational Pedagogy & Cognitive Apprenticeship (36 hrs)
  - *Module CTI-102:* Cyber Range Simulation & Lab Management (36 hrs)
  - *Module CTI-103:* Rubric-Based Evaluation & Practical Exam Proctoring (36 hrs)
  - *Module CTI-104:* Training Ethics, Anti-Harassment & Civil Rights (36 hrs)
* Serves as an institutional career safety valve, qualifying Journeymen and Masters to earn union scale teaching JATC community college cohorts between enterprise contracts or during macroeconomic downturns.
