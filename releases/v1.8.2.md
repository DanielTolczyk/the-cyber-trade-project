**Release Title:** Regional Hubs, FIFO Dispatch & Clearinghouse Invariants

### Executive Summary

Release **v1.8.2** establishes the operational and governance link between the National Clearinghouse Registry, the Craft Guild Dispatch Hall, and regional multi-employer labor standards. This release formalizes the Three-Book Out-of-Work Dispatch hierarchy, codifies strict First-In, First-Out (FIFO) chronological referral seniority to prevent arbitrary bypass or blacklisting, establishes the 5 Geographic Regional District Hubs and Local Chapters, enforces the Remote Anti-Wage-Arbitrage Compensation Rule, and introduces cross-ledger artifact deduplication and real-time PKI certificate revocation standards.

---

### Key Architectural & Governance Ratifications

#### 1. The Three-Book Guild Dispatch Standard & FIFO Referral Seniority
* **Priority Safe Harbor (Top Priority):** Guarantees immediate, protected referral for displaced apprentices resulting from employer insolvency or contract termination, and engineers transferred following substantiated Form FORM-001 technical refusals.
* **Book 1 (Resident Local Members):** Primary referral queue for fully licensed Journeymen and Registered Apprentices residing within that Local Chapter jurisdiction, dispatched strictly by chronological out-of-work registration date (longest searching dispatched first).
* **Book 2 (Regional Travelers):** Reciprocal referral queue for credentialed practitioners registered in sibling Locals traveling into the District, activated when local Book 1 labor is fully absorbed.
* **Anti-Blacklisting & Turn-Down Protections:** Sponsoring employers may not arbitrarily reject dispatched practitioners; rejections require an objective operational non-conformance statement submitted within 48 hours. Practitioners may decline up to two dispatch offers without queue penalty.

#### 2. Five Geographic Regional Districts & COLI Zone Classifications
* **District 1 (Local 101 - Mid-Atlantic & Northeast Hub):** DC, MD, VA, DE, PA, NY, NJ, New England (Tier A COLI - $85.00/hr RJPB Baseline).
* **District 2 (Local 204 - Midwest & Great Lakes Hub):** IL, IN, OH, MI, WI, MN, IA, MO (Tier B COLI - $72.00/hr RJPB Baseline).
* **District 3 (Local 302 - Pacific Northwest & West Coast Hub):** WA, OR, CA, NV, ID, AK, HI (Tier A COLI - $92.00/hr RJPB Baseline).
* **District 4 (Local 405 - South & Gulf Coast Hub):** TX, LA, MS, AL, GA, FL, TN, NC, SC (Tier B COLI - $78.00/hr RJPB Baseline).
* **District 5 (Local 508 - Mountain West & Plains Hub):** CO, UT, AZ, NM, WY, MT, ND, SD, NE, KS (Tier C COLI - $62.00/hr RJPB Baseline).

#### 3. Remote Work & Anti-Wage-Arbitrage Compensation Rule
* Eliminates geographic wage undercutting by mandating that remote operational shifts are compensated at the higher of the sponsoring employer's headquarters Local Chapter RJPB rate or the practitioner's home residence Local Chapter RJPB rate.

#### 4. Cross-Ledger Artifact Collision & Deduplication Invariant
* Prevents fraudulent double-counting across logbooks: an individual immutable artifact reference (`git_commit_hash`, `change_ticket_id`, `incident_record_id`) cannot be credited across multiple solo shifts without verified joint pairing metadata.

#### 5. Real-Time PKI Certificate Revocation (PKI-CRL)
* Automates instantaneous trade key revocation and initiates 24-hour notification to bound PEC employers and CUAAC cyber insurance underwriters upon any Master of Record signing authority suspension.
