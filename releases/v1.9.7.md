### Executive Summary

Release **v1.9.7** ratifies the canonical **Employer Direct Browsing Prohibition (The Intermediary Invariant)**, formalizes the role of the **Talent Clearinghouse Dispatch Officer** as the mandatory neutral intermediary between participating employers and out-of-work practitioners, codifies **Active Master Practitioner & Master of Record (MoR) Dispatch Sub-Ledgers**, establishes the **30-Day Queue Aging Alert & Proactive Outreach Protocol**, and synchronizes the complete six-tier trade qualification hierarchy across the `cyber-trade-clearinghouse` engine, test suites, and demonstration portal.

---

### Key Architectural & Governance Ratifications

#### 1. Employer Direct Browsing Prohibition & The Intermediary Invariant (`governance/operational-field-roles.md`)
* Codified the **Mandatory Intermediary Invariant**: Participating employers submit formal Labor Requisitions detailing operational role requirements (trade tier, specialty endorsement, security clearance, and MoR statutory sign-off authority).
* Direct employer searching, resume scraping, or filtering of member profiles from Out-of-Work Books is **strictly prohibited under Guild labor covenants** to prevent blacklisting, wage suppression, and arbitrary screening bias.
* The neutral **Talent Clearinghouse Dispatch Officer** validates incoming requisitions against collective bargaining standards, evaluates candidates under strict First-In, First-Out (FIFO) chronological availability, conducts bilateral fit assessments, and issues the official **Dispatch Referral Slip**.

#### 2. Active Master Practitioner & Master of Record (MoR) Dispatch Protocols
* Established specialized dispatch sub-ledgers for licensed Master Practitioners registering under three distinct operational categories:
  * **Full-Time Master of Record:** Dedicated operational appointments accepting statutory sign-off authority and exclusive standing to issue formal Notices of Safety Non-Concurrence (Form FORM-001).
  * **Fractional Master of Record (vMoR):** Regulated retainers for SMBs and trade contractors subject to strict statutory account caps (maximum 8 Tier-III SMBs or 3 Tier-II regulated enterprises).
  * **General Advisory & Principal Engineering:** Master Practitioners providing architectural review and mentorship without carrying corporate statutory sign-off liability.
* Enabled participating enterprises to issue dedicated **MoR Labor Requisitions** to satisfy Pillar VII underwriter warranty compliance requirements.

#### 3. Queue Aging Alerts & Proactive Outreach Protocols
* Codified mandatory **Queue Aging Alerts** triggered when any practitioner reaches thirty (30) or more consecutive days on active Out-of-Work books.
* Mandated proactive bilateral intervention by the Dispatch Officer to investigate friction factors (modality preferences, niche endorsements, or COLI boundaries), explore inter-district traveler opportunities (Book 2), or coordinate JATC skills enhancement with Regional Training Directors.

#### 4. Ecosystem Clearinghouse Synchronization (`cyber-trade-clearinghouse`)
* **Six-Tier Qualification Hierarchy:** Extended the clearinghouse matching engine to support exact tier matching across all six trade levels:
  * Tier 1 Apprentice (Entry - 50% RJPB)
  * Tier 2 Apprentice (Progressing - 60% RJPB)
  * Tier 3 Apprentice (Intermediate - 70% RJPB)
  * Tier 4 Apprentice (Advanced - 80% RJPB)
  * Licensed Journeyman (Full Trade Licensure - 100% RJPB)
  * Master Practitioner (Board Defense Licensure - 135% to 150% RJPB)
* **Two-Sided Dispatch Ledger:** Added formal `DispatchRequisition` and `DispatchReferralSlip` data models to the Python engine (`cyber_trade_clearinghouse/models.py`) and registry ledger (`registry.py`).
* **Dispatch Officer Console:** Transformed the client-side portal (`clearinghouse/public/index.html` and `app.js`) from a unilateral selector into an interactive **Dispatch Officer Intermediary Console** with an active Employer Labor Requisitions ledger, queue aging badges, and MoR verification stamps.
* **Test Suite Expansion:** Added comprehensive unit tests in `tests/test_dispatch.py` verifying multi-tier dispatch matching, MoR candidacy segregation, and the end-to-end Dispatch Officer referral workflow (19/19 tests passing).
