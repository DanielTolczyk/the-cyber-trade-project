## What's New in v1.8.0

### 1. Essential Services Labor Accord & Systemic Resilience
* **Critical Infrastructure Strike Prohibition & Binding Arbitration:** Formalized Section 12 in `governance/guild-formation-and-charter.md` establishing statutory no-strike pacts for Tier 1 life-safety, healthcare, energy grid, and national security environments, backed by mandatory binding interest arbitration to resolve labor disputes without operational downtime.
* **Emergency Surge Mobilization:** Codified Guild Surge Hall protocols guaranteeing continuous incident response defense during national cybersecurity emergencies while preserving worker rest cycles and overtime compensation.

### 2. Open Source Supply Chain Integrity & Upstream Security Accords
* **Targeted OSS Security Fork Exemption (`templates/FORM-005_targeted-oss-security-fork-exemption.md`):** Standardized legal and operational instruments for maintaining temporary emergency security patches in open-source dependencies when upstream maintainers are unresponsive.
* **Dual-Licensing & Safe Harbors:** Codified clear disclosure timelines (30 days) and indemnification shields for maintainers and practitioners contributing upstream defensive security fixes in `framework/contractors-and-consulting.md` and `framework/velocity-and-cloud.md`.

### 3. Operational Edge Cases & Practical Exam Remediation
* **60-Day Modular Retake Window:** Formalized remediation protocol in `framework/apprenticeship-standards.md` allowing apprentice candidates who achieve >=80% on four domain stations but fail a single station to re-test only the deficient station within 60 days.
* **Disaster Ratio Relief:** Codified temporary supervisory ratio relief in `framework/supervisory-ratios.md` during officially declared catastrophic cyber incidents.
* **Whistleblower Anti-Retaliation Safe Harbors:** Reinforced Section 5 Right of Technical Refusal protections in `framework/technical-refusal-and-liability.md` and `framework/code-of-ethics-and-conduct.md`.

### 4. Interactive Portal & Documentation Architecture
* **Portal Search & Navigation:** Added client-side full-text search filtering for cataloged glossary terms and standardized responsive reading typography.
* **FAQ Expansion (FAQ 29 - 32):** Added detailed explainers on essential services agreements, open-source security forks, modular re-testing, and emergency ratio relief.
