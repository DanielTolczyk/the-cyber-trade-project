### Executive Summary

Release **v1.9.8** addresses critical implementation findings identified during architectural evaluation. It codifies the explicit **Liability-Supervision Link** between mandatory 2:1 supervisory ratios and statutory liability transfer under Pillar V, formalizes the **Tier 4 Elective Specialty Hour Substitution & Minimum Generalist Floors**, establishes the bipartite **JATC Specialty Elective Petition & Approval Workflow**, and updates the canonical **Universal Logbook Schema** with the `specialty_endorsement_code` telemetry attribute to enable direct post-licensure articulation.

### Key Architectural & Governance Ratifications

#### 1. Supervisory Ratio Breach & Mandatory Liability Transfer (`pillars/05_personal-liability-and-refusal.md`, `framework/technical-refusal-and-liability.md`, `framework/supervisory-ratios.md`)
* **Unlawful Operational Directive:** Codified that compelling practitioners to operate in breach of statutory supervisory headcount ratios (`supervisory-ratios.md`) or forcing solo-apprentice operational shifts constitutes an unlawful unsafe operational order.
* **Affirmative Master of Record Duty:** Established the Master of Record's affirmative professional obligation to issue a formal Notice of Safety Non-Concurrence (Form FORM-001) upon any corporate directive requiring frontline defensive operations out of ratio after a 72-hour cure period following frontline Exception Flags (Form FORM-003).
* **Total Executive Liability Shift:** Mandated that executive leadership may only compel out-of-ratio operations by executing an Executive Override & Risk Acceptance Form (Form FORM-002). Execution of Form FORM-002 transfers 100% of civil, regulatory, and malpractice liabilities arising from breaches, undetected intrusions, data losses, or operational failures during the non-compliant operational period exclusively to the overriding corporate officers.
* **Frontline Malpractice Safe Harbor:** Guaranteed unconditional statutory malpractice immunity under the Trade Liability Shield for supervising Journeymen and registered Apprentices compelled to work during active Form FORM-002 ratio overrides.

#### 2. Tier 4 Elective Specialty Hour Substitution & Minimum Generalist Floors (`framework/apprenticeship-standards.md`)
* **Strict Minimum Domain Floors:** Formally established that no apprentice may graduate as a Licensed Journeyman with fewer than **1,000 verified operational hours in any individual core domain**.
* **Domain Substitution Caps:** Defined exact substitution allowances for the 1,000 Tier 4 elective hours:
  * **Domain 2 (Detection / SOC):** Maximum 500 hours substituted (preserving a mandatory 1,500-hour floor).
  * **Domains 1, 3, 4, and 5:** Maximum 250 hours substituted per domain (preserving a mandatory 1,250-hour floor in each).

#### 3. JATC Specialty Elective Approval & Articulation Workflow (`framework/specialty-endorsements.md`)
* **Bipartite Approval Petition:** Codified the formal **Tier 4 Specialty Elective Petition** in the Universal Logbook, requiring dual cryptographic approval by the Sponsoring Master of Record (confirming production facility alignment and Journeyman mentorship) and the Regional JATC Training Director (confirming academic progress and clean ethical standing).
* **Accelerated 50% Post-Licensure Credit:** Codified that the 1,000 verified Tier 4 specialty hours apply immediately as an accelerated **50% advance credit** against the 2,000-hour post-licensure requirement for that Specialty Endorsement upon earning Journeyman licensure.

#### 4. Universal Logbook Schema & Telemetry Synchronization (`framework/logbook-standards.md`, `framework/logbook-schema.md`)
* **Specialty Telemetry Tag:** Added the canonical `specialty_endorsement_code` attribute to the Logbook Entry Schema, allowing automated tracking of specialized rotations in parallel with generalist domain hours.
* **Search Index Freshness:** Recompiled the static search index across all 702 documentation sections.
