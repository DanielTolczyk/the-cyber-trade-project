### Executive Summary

Release **v1.9.5** ratifies **Form FORM-008 (Autonomous AI System Deployment & Tool Execution Clearance)**, codifies strict **Automated Tool Equipment Liability & Containment Standards** under Pillar V and Pillar VII, reinforces human exclusivity in supervisory ratios (classifying AI tools as non-personnel equipment), synchronizes the **SE-AIML** specialty track under the 5-domain generalist foundation, and promotes the release management archive from `docs/releases/` to the canonical top-level `releases/` root directory.

### Key Architectural & Governance Ratifications

#### 1. Form FORM-008 Ratification & Autonomous Tool Containment (`templates/FORM-008_ai-agent-safety-clearance.md`)
* Codified **Form FORM-008** as the standardized procedural instrument executed by an active Master of Record (MoR) to clear production AI/ML products and autonomous agentic workflows.
* Established deterministic containment requirements: bounded least-privilege scoping, semantic firewalls, human-in-the-loop confirmation gates for high-privilege mutations, and an out-of-band emergency kill switch.
* Enforced a mandatory **180-day maximum clearance validity window**, requiring periodic re-certification of adversarial robustness against MITRE ATLAS threat profiles.
* Established a dual-signoff pathway requiring technical review by an accredited `SE-AIML` specialist when an enterprise MoR does not personally hold the endorsement.

#### 2. Automated Tool Liability & Equipment Classification (`framework/technical-refusal-and-liability.md`)
* **Zero Legal Personhood Invariant:** Formally codified that software tools, machine learning models, and autonomous agents possess zero legal personhood, independent agency, or statutory standing. They are classified strictly as enterprise operational equipment.
* **Absolute Enterprise Responsibility:** The deploying enterprise bears 100% legal, regulatory, and financial liability for all network actions, API queries, or damages originating from its compute infrastructure or credentials. Claiming autonomous tool non-determinism is invalid as a defense.
* **Uninspected Equipment Operation & Actuarial Exclusion:** Operating autonomous execution tools outside sandboxed containment without an active FORM-008 is classified as uninspected equipment operation, invalidating cyber insurance due-care safe harbors under CUAAC underwriting baselines.
* **Executive Override on Uncontained Tooling:** If corporate officers override a Master of Record refusal to clear uncontained autonomous agents, all resulting statutory and civil liability transfers exclusively to the overriding officers under Form FORM-002.

#### 3. Human Supervisory Ratio Exclusivity (`framework/supervisory-ratios.md`)
* Codified that automated tools, LLMs, and agentic workflows are enterprise equipment, not labor.
* Prohibited AI tools from counting toward headcount ratios or substituting for human Journey-level supervision under the mandatory 2:1 on-shift operational ratio.
* Mandated that registered apprentice seats and JATC training trust quotas cannot be replaced or reduced through the deployment of autonomous software automation.

#### 4. Specialty Track Curriculum & Generalist Domain Symmetry (`framework/specialty-endorsements.md`, `framework/specialty-tracks/ai-machine-learning.md`)
* Aligned the `SE-AIML` prerequisite structure to the 5 mandatory generalist core domains (8,000 verified operational hours).
* Replaced legacy nominalizations with active NIST-grade verbs across work role definitions and domain rubrics.
* Embedded FORM-008 into MoR statutory authority for enterprise AI deployments.

#### 5. Framework Directory Architecture Refactor (`releases/`)
* Promoted the release archive from `docs/releases/` to the canonical top-level `releases/` root directory (`releases/v1.8.0.md` through `releases/v1.9.5.md`).
* Updated automated release tooling (`scripts/release.py`), specification validators (`scripts/validate-specifications.py`), and preview generators (`scripts/build_preview.py`) to reflect the new directory structure.
* Synchronized interactive search indexing and portal navigation.
