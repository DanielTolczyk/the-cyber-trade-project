### Executive Summary

Release **v1.9.6** ratifies the canonical **Leave, Benefit Standards & Multi-Employer FMLA Equivalency Standard** (`framework/leave-and-benefit-standards.md`), establishes the **Taft-Hartley Paid Family & Medical Leave (PFML) & Disability Sub-Trust** funded at $0.75 per labor hour, introduces multi-employer portable leave parity for 1099 independent contractors, codifies NCTB apprentice logbook pause-and-resume protections, integrates multi-employer fringe benefit schedules ($14.75 per hour total package), and enforces strict structural taxonomy guardrails eliminating overloaded terms across governance and operational charters.

### Key Architectural & Governance Ratifications

#### 1. Leave & Benefit Standards Ratification (`framework/leave-and-benefit-standards.md`)
* Codified **Leave, Benefit Standards & Multi-Employer FMLA Equivalency** establishing a two-division jurisdictional boundary:
  * **Division A (Employer-Retained):** Direct payroll obligations for casual PTO, incidental sick leave (10 to 15 days/year), and standard post-incident DFIR 14-hour shift rest cycles (under 5 consecutive business days).
  * **Division B (Multi-Employer Trust):** Taft-Hartley § 302(c) ERISA fiduciary pool covering leaves of 5 or more consecutive business days, providing 12 weeks of Paid Parental Leave and up to 26 weeks of Short-Term Disability (STD) at 70% of active baseline wage step (capped at 70% RJPB).
* Established the **Unified 1,000-Hour Qualification Threshold**, eliminating single-employer tenure cliffs and extending full statutory leave protection to practitioners across startups, regional MSSPs, and boutique defense firms.
* Integrated the **Hour-Bank Medical Premium Continuation**, maintaining active family health and welfare coverage during approved parental and medical leaves without out-of-pocket premiums.
* Defined the **Pause-and-Resume Apprentice Invariant**, protecting registered apprentices on medical or parental leave against forfeiture of verified logbook hours, domain competencies, or active wage-step seniority.

#### 2. Multi-Employer Fringe Benefit Package & PFML Sub-Trust (`framework/wage-scales.md`, `governance/dues-and-trust-economics.md`)
* Formally codified the **Multi-Employer Fringe Benefit Remittance Package** totaling **$14.75 per labor hour worked**:
  * JATC Training Trust: $1.00 / hr worked (Journeyman and Master hours)
  * Health & Welfare Trust: $7.00 / hr worked (All practitioners)
  * PFML & Disability Sub-Trust: $0.75 / hr worked (All practitioners)
  * Defined Benefit Pension Trust: $6.00 / hr worked (Journeyman and Master hours)
* Established state statutory integration rules (wrap-around insurer model), ensuring the Guild Trust supplements state programs (e.g., CA, NY, WA, MA, NJ, CO, OR) up to the 70% baseline trade wage standard.

#### 3. 1099 Contractor Benefit Portability & Parity (`framework/contractors-and-consulting.md`)
* Ratified the four parity mechanisms for independent contractors and solo consultancies:
  * **Loaded Wage Multiplier (1.35x - 1.45x)** factoring in self-employment tax obligations and personal leave reserves.
  * **Vacation & Sick Trust Sub-Accounts (Hourly Stamp)** of $3.50 per hour worked.
  * **Equal Trust Access** to the 12-week parental and 26-week disability benefit pool upon logging 1,000 verified hours across signatory contracts in a 12-month period.
  * **Clearinghouse Employer-of-Record (EOR) Bridge** allowing solo practitioners to deliver work as W-2 employees of record.

#### 4. Employer Participation Agreement Synchronization (`templates/FORM-007_employer-participation-agreement.md`)
* Updated mandatory signatory covenants in **Form FORM-007** to require compliance with trust remittance schedules:
  * Health & Welfare Trust ($7.00 / hr worked)
  * PFML & Disability Sub-Trust ($0.75 / hr worked)
  * Defined Benefit Pension Trust ($6.00 / hr worked)
* Codified mandatory job restoration rights and NCTB Pause-and-Resume protections for returning practitioners.

#### 5. Strict Structural Taxonomy Hygiene & Guardrail Enforcement
* Eliminated taxonomy collisions across governance and operational charters:
  * Enforced "Constituency" for Tripartite Board caucuses in `governance/board-structure.md` and `governance/board-elections-and-bylaws.md`, preserving "Pillar" strictly for the 7 Immutable Core Pillars.
  * Corrected institutional entity references in `governance/transition-plan.md` and `governance/underwriter-and-actuarial-consortium.md`.
  * Updated `GLOSSARY.md` and `assets/js/portal.js` hover tooltips with newly introduced statutory and benefit terms (`Multi-Employer FMLA Equivalency`, `Hour-Bank System`, `Loaded Trade Rate`).
  * Rebuilt `assets/js/search-index.json` to synchronize full-text search indexing across all modified documents.
