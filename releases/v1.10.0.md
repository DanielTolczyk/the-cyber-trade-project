### Executive Summary

Release **v1.10.0** establishes a major empirical and governance milestone for the Cybersecurity Trade Project. It formalizes the **Persistent Labor Shortage Index (PLSI)** mathematical telemetry specification, codifies the **Sustained Deficit Rule** governing JATC core curriculum adjustments under the statutory 18-Month Curriculum Notice Rule, ratifies the **Supervised Specialty Trainee Dispatch** pathway to resolve the industry experience paradox without diluting independent licensure rigor, mandates the **Real-World Precedent Mapping Mandate** across all specifications and AI policies, and deploys an interactive shortage evaluation desk and dispatch advisory suite across the Guild Dispatch Hall.

### Key Architectural & Governance Ratifications

#### 1. Persistent Labor Shortage Index (PLSI) Mathematical Specification (`framework/apprenticeship-standards.md`, `framework/specialty-endorsements.md`)
* **Empirical Shortage Ratio:** Codified the mathematical formula evaluated quarterly by the JATC Talent Clearinghouse across all specialty endorsement tracks:
  $$\text{PLSI} = \left(\frac{R_{\ge 30}}{P_{\text{spec}}}\right) \times 100$$
  Where $R_{\ge 30}$ represents unfilled Participating Employer Council (PEC) requisitions open $\ge 30$ calendar days and $P_{\text{spec}}$ represents the active qualified practitioner pool.
* **Statutory Shortage Classifications:** Established objective tripartite thresholds:
  * **Structural Deficit ($\text{PLSI} \ge 20\%$ for $\ge 3$ consecutive quarters):** Authorizes JATC core curriculum reviews, laboratory hardware subsidies, and expedited traveler dispatch (Book 2 / Book 3 cross-jurisdictional referrals).
  * **Transient Demand Spike ($\text{PLSI} \ge 20\%$ for $< 3$ consecutive quarters):** Normalized market variance absorbed strictly through modular continuing education electives; barred from disrupting multi-year core rotational baselines.
  * **Balanced Supply ($\text{PLSI} < 20\%$):** Standard labor market equilibrium.

#### 2. Two-Tier Curriculum Governance & The Sustained Deficit Rule (`framework/apprenticeship-standards.md`, `governance/guild-formation-and-charter.md`)
* **Structural Curriculum Separation:** Formalized clear statutory boundaries separating short-term modular continuing education (16 to 40 hour elective lab sessions for active Journeymen and unassigned trainees) from the foundational 8,000-hour registered apprenticeship standards.
* **The Sustained Deficit Rule:** Bound the National Board and JATCs to require sustained deficits ($\text{PLSI} \ge 20\%$ across 3 consecutive quarters) before initiating formal Related Technical Instruction (RTI) or rotational reallocation under the statutory **18-Month Curriculum Notice Rule**, permanently safeguarding active apprentice cohorts against curriculum churn and technology hype cycles.

#### 3. Supervised Specialty Trainee Dispatch Architecture (`framework/specialty-endorsements.md`, `framework/apprenticeship-standards.md`)
* **Experience Paradox Resolution:** Resolved the industry trap where practitioners cannot secure specialty roles without prior experience.
* **Non-Conferral of Licensure Invariant:** Explicitly codified that 16-to-40-hour modular laboratory courses award verified Continuing Education Units (CEUs) and unlock **Supervised Specialty Trainee** referral standing on the Out-of-Work Register, but **never confer full Specialty Endorsements (`SE-XXXX`)**.
* **Supervised Specialty Referral:** Sponsoring employers with persistent deficits may engage Supervised Specialty Trainees provided all operational shifts occur under the line-of-sight supervision of an active Master of Record holding that endorsement.
* **Logbook Hour Accrual:** All shifts logged under the designated Master accrue verified specialty runtime in the Universal Logbook toward the 2,000 post-licensure operational hours required for full endorsement conferral.

#### 4. The Real-World Precedent Mapping Mandate (`AGENTS.md`, `.clinerules`, `scripts/validate-specifications.py`)
* **The Anti-Invention Invariant:** Mandated that all labor, training, referral, and governance mechanisms must map 1:1 to established statutory and trade precedents (Title 29 CFR Parts 29 and 30, Taft-Hartley / LMRA Section 302(c), ERISA, standard JATCs such as IBEW/NECA and UA, State PE Practice Acts, and BLS standards).
* **Zero Synthetic Neologisms:** Strictly prohibited invented pseudo-academic jargon, corporate consulting models, and nominalized buzzwords.
* **Expanded Quality Gate Linter:** Updated `scripts/validate-specifications.py` to automatically block consulting framework patterns and synthetic noun clusters.

#### 5. Interactive Guild Dispatch Hall Suite (`guild-hall`)
* **Interactive Shortage Evaluation Desk:** Deployed segmented category filters (`All Specialties`, `Structural Deficit (>= 20%)`, `Transient Spikes`, `Balanced Supply`) on the JATC Training & Market Index view with dynamic table row filtering.
* **Modular Lab Enrollment Modal:** Implemented an in-app confirmation modal detailing module hours, $0 trust-funded tuition, and statutory disclosures regarding supervised trainee referral vs. independent licensure.
* **Dynamic Requisition Labor Advisory:** Automated real-time advisory banners in employer labor requisitions when selecting persistent-deficit specialties (`SE-CLD`, `SE-ICS`), alerting employers to expedited traveler referral and supervised trainee dispatch eligibility.
