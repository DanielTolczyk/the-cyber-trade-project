### Executive Summary

Release **v1.9.9** codifies essential institutional delegation boundaries and fiduciary plan governance across the Cybersecurity Trade Project specifications. It establishes the **Pension Fiduciary Plan Document & Actuarial Calibration Boundary** under ERISA § 403 / § 103, defines the **Supplemental Unemployment Benefit (SUB) Fund** auxiliary administration structure, formalizes strict **Emergency Strike & Hardship Reserve** disbursement controls, defines the **Board Practical Challenge Examination** cost-recovery funding model, and establishes the **International Mutual Recognition Boundary** governing cross-border licensure reciprocity.

---

### Key Architectural & Governance Ratifications

#### 1. Pension Fiduciary Plan Document & Actuarial Calibration Boundary (`governance/dues-and-trust-economics.md`, `docs/systemic-edge-cases-and-defense.md`)
* **Plan Document Delegation:** Formally codified that specific benefit accrual unit formulas, hurdle rates, Normal Retirement Age (NRA, age 65), Early Retirement Age (ERA, age 55 with 10 service credit years), and Qualified Joint and Survivor Annuity (QJSA) conversion factors are not hardcoded into trade collective bargaining agreements.
* **ERISA Section 103 Actuarial Certification:** Delegated these parameters to the authoritative *Taft-Hartley Pension Trust Agreement and Summary Plan Description (SPD)*, maintained and adjusted triennially by the 50/50 Bipartisan Board of Trustees under independent Enrolled Actuary (EA/FSA) certification.
* **$0 UVB Invariant Preservation:** Barred static dollar benefit guarantees in core governance to maintain the 100%+ funded status and $0 Unfunded Vested Benefit (UVB) invariant of the Variable Annuity Pension Plan (VAPP), protecting signatory employers from MPPAA withdrawal liabilities.

#### 2. Supplemental Unemployment Benefit (SUB) Fund Administration (`governance/dues-and-trust-economics.md`)
* **Fiduciary Architecture:** Codified the SUB Fund as an auxiliary stabilization sub-trust under the Taft-Hartley Health & Welfare fiduciary pool (ERISA Section 403).
* **Trustee Rulemaking Authority:** Clarified that weekly supplemental unemployment caps, qualification thresholds, and duration limits are calibrated and maintained directly by the 50/50 Bipartisan Board of Trustees pursuant to master bargaining agreements and federal Department of Labor multi-employer trust guidelines.

#### 3. Emergency Strike & Hardship Reserve Disbursement Controls (`governance/dues-and-trust-economics.md`)
* **Governance Oversight:** Codified strict disbursement rules for the 10% Emergency Strike & Hardship Reserve under National Guild Executive Council bylaws.
* **Supermajority & Audit Controls:** Mandated that strike or lockout stipends require a two-thirds (2/3) supermajority authorization of the Executive Council, while emergency disaster hardship relief grants are administered by the Member Relief Committee and subject to monthly review and reconciliation by elected rank-and-file Local Chapter Audit Committees.

#### 4. Practical Examination Cyber Range Cost-Recovery Model (`framework/licensure-and-progression.md`)
* **Trust & Grant Subsidies:** Codified that air-gapped cyber range infrastructure and automated scoring for the Board Practical Challenge Examination are subsidized through JATC Training Trust remittances ($1.00/hour worked) and public workforce development grants, with employers absorbing sitting costs for registered apprentices.
* **Anti-Profiteering Cap:** Established that any nominal sitting fees assessed to lateral independent candidates challenging the examination are strictly regulated by the National Cybersecurity Trade Board (NCTB) on a transparent, non-profit cost-recovery basis, prohibiting commercial testing vendor markup.

#### 5. International Licensure Reciprocity Boundary (`governance/model-legislation-and-compact.md`)
* **State vs. International Demarcation:** Codified that the Interstate Cybersecurity Practice Compact (ICPC) applies strictly among signatory states of the United States.
* **Bilateral Treaty Ratification:** Clarified that professional reciprocity with foreign allied frameworks (such as the Canadian Red Seal, UK Cyber Scheme, or Australian Cyber Security Accreditation) is evaluated, negotiated, and formally ratified by the National Cybersecurity Trade Board (NCTB) through bilateral accords, preventing automatic domestic Compact privileges without NCTB Clearinghouse verification.
