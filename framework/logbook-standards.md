# Universal Logbook Standards & Competency Ledger

The Universal Digital Logbook provides an immutable, verifiable record of a practitioner's hands-on experience, eliminating resume inflation and redundant hiring assessments.

---

## 1. Logbook Entry Schema
Every operational entry submitted by an apprentice must include:
* **Timestamp & Duration:** Exact hours dedicated to the operational task.
* **NICE Framework Work Role Alignment:** Mapped to standardized NIST NICE specialty areas (e.g., PR-CDO-001, OM-NET-001).
* **Technical Domain Classification:** IAM, Network Defense, Systems Hardening, Incident Response, or GRC.
* **Artifact Reference / Ticket ID:** Anonymized, non-sensitive reference verifying the completed work.
* **Journeyman Digital Sign-Off:** Cryptographic signature and license number of the supervising Journeyman.

---

## 2. Portability & Privacy
* **Practitioner Ownership:** The logbook is owned by the individual practitioner, not their employer.
* **Sanitized Records:** Log entries record technical proficiencies and hours without disclosing proprietary corporate data, client names, or classified details.
* **Instant Verification:** Prospective employers and licensing boards verify accredited hours via a public ledger API.
