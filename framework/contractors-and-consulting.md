# Independent Consulting, Contracting & The Licensed Trade Contractor Model

To support agile enterprise staffing, enable professional autonomy, and establish a viable commercial ecosystem for small-to-medium businesses (SMBs), the trade framework separates **individual professional licensure** from **corporate employment classification**.

This standard defines the rules governing 1099/W-2 contractors, independent consultants, and the establishment of **Licensed Cybersecurity Trade Contractor** business entities (LLCs, partnerships, MSSPs, and boutique consultancies).

---

## 1. The Employment Classification Matrix

The framework accommodates four primary practitioner engagement models while maintaining strict supervision and training integrity:

| Engagement Model | Legal Classification | Licensing & Supervision Prerequisite | Insurance & Telemetry Gating |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Registered Apprentice** | **Direct W-2 Only**<br>(Employer of Record or JATC Trust) | Must practice under the direct supervision of a licensed Journeyman/Master (2:1 maximum on-shift ratio). | Banned from 1099 classification under labor misclassification rules; telemetry signed by supervising mentor. |
| **Staff Augmentation Journeyman** | **W-2 (Agency) or 1099 Subcontractor** | Holds active, verifiable Journeyman License in the National Clearinghouse Registry. | Registry ID mapped into client telemetry; satisfies enterprise insurance underwriting staffing ratios. |
| **Independent Security Consultant** | **1099 Independent Contractor / Sole Proprietor** | Holds active Journeyman or Master Practitioner credential. | Carries individual trade E&O coverage; performs unbundled assessments, pen-testing, or engineering tasks. |
| **Licensed Cybersecurity Trade Contractor** | **Commercial Entity**<br>(LLC, Inc., Partnership, MSSP) | Must designate a qualifying **Master of Record (MoR)** holding an active Master Practitioner license. | Holds Commercial Surety Bond & Contractor E&O; authorized to sponsor apprentices and execute statutory safety sign-offs. |

## 2. Apprentice Labor Protections & 1099 Prohibition

To prevent the exploitation of early-career talent and comply with legal wage and labor standards:

* **Strict 1099 Prohibition:** Registered Apprentices **cannot** be classified as 1099 independent contractors. Because apprentices legally operate in a structured training capacity requiring direct oversight and supervisory gating, independent contractor status constitutes misclassification.
* **The Multi-Employer JATC Clearinghouse:** If a client enterprise or boutique consulting firm requires flexible, project-based apprentice labor without adding permanent headcount to their internal payroll, they contract with the **Guild JATC Hiring Hall / Multi-Employer Training Trust**. The JATC acts as the statutory W-2 employer of record, administering wages, benefits, and classroom instruction, while dispatching apprentices to host employers.

## 3. Establishing a Licensed Cybersecurity Trade Contractor (LLC / Consulting Firm)

### Mandatory Entity Prerequisites
To achieve accredited status and issue certified compliance sign-offs, a consulting firm must maintain:

1. **Qualifying Master of Record (MoR):** The firm must designate at least one full-time equity partner or W-2 executive who holds an active **Master Practitioner license (12,000+ verified runtime hours)**.
2. **Commercial Surety & E&O Bonding:** The entity must carry active commercial Trade Errors & Omissions (E&O) insurance and a trade surety bond calibrated to its operational contract volume.
3. **Registry Accreditation:** The firm maintains an active listing in the National Cybersecurity Trade Board Registry, enabling prospective clients and cyber underwriters to verify entity compliance in real time.

## 4. Subcontracting & The Fractional Master of Record (vMoR for SMBs)

Small and medium-sized businesses often lack the capital to employ a full-time, in-house Master Practitioner. The Trade Contractor model enables **Fractional Master of Record (vMoR)** services to bridge the defense gap:

* **Statutory Scope:** An accredited Trade Contractor may be retained by an SMB to perform regular architectural reviews, audit logbook telemetry for in-house staff, and execute formal **Statutory Safety Sign-Offs** required by insurers or regulatory mandates (e.g., FTC Safeguards Rule, HIPAA, CMMC).
* **Active Sign-Off Responsibility:** When a fractional Master of Record signs off on a client deployment, they assume statutory technical accountability for that specific scope, backed by the firm's commercial E&O bond.
* **Refusal Standing:** A fractional MoR possesses identical legal standing to issue a formal **Notice of Safety Non-Concurrence** if an SMB client overrides critical baseline standards, instantly transferring liability to the client's executive officers and notifying cyber underwriters.

## 5. Capacity Ratios for Consulting & Service Providers

To prevent consulting firms and MSSPs from overextending senior staff and diluting oversight quality across dozens of client accounts, the trade enforces **Contractor Capacity Caps**:

* **Apprentice Sponsoring Threshold:** A consulting firm must employ at least one full-time Journeyman or Master on staff for every two registered apprentices it hosts (`2:1 ratio`).
* **Fractional MoR Account Limits:** A single Master of Record operating in a fractional consulting capacity may serve as the designated MoR for a **maximum of 5 concurrent small-business accounts** or **1 mid-tier enterprise account**, ensuring adequate operational depth and line-of-sight oversight.
