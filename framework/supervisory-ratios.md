# Supervisory Ratios & Operational Headcount Standards

Supervisory ratios ensure quality mentorship, system stability, operational safety, and sustainable workload distribution across enterprise teams, security operations centers (SOCs), managed service providers (MSSPs), and independent consulting practices.

---

## 1. Standard Supervisory Ratio Table

| Environment / Work Role | Max Ratio (Apprentice : Journeyman) | Minimum Master Practitioner Oversight | Scope & Setting |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **MSSP / Managed Multi-Tenant Operations** | **2 : 1** (per client tenant tier) | Mandatory licensed oversight on all active shifts | Multi-tenant monitoring & customer triage |

## 2. Consulting Firm & Trade Contractor Capacity Limits

To prevent boutique consultancies, MSSPs, and contractors from overextending senior staff across multiple client engagements, the trade enforces entity-level capacity limits:

* **Contractor Apprentice Cap:** A Licensed Cybersecurity Trade Contractor entity must employ at least one full-time Journeyman or Master Practitioner on staff for every two registered apprentices hosted (`2:1 ratio`).
* **Risk-Weighted Fractional MoR (vMoR) Ceiling:** An individual Master Practitioner acting as a fractional Master of Record for small-to-medium businesses (SMBs) may hold the designated MoR title for a **maximum of eight (8) Tier-III commercial SMB accounts** or **three (3) Tier-II regulated enterprise accounts**. Serving beyond these limits requires appointing an additional licensed Master Practitioner within the firm. Fractional MoR assignments are strictly prohibited for Tier-I Critical Infrastructure.
* **MSSP Tenant Throttling:** In multi-tenant managed defense environments, a single supervising Journeyman may not supervise apprentices across more than **5 concurrent active customer production feeds** simultaneously.

## 3. Operational Boundaries & Velocity Exclusions

* **Human-in-the-Loop Scope Only:** Supervisory headcount ratios apply **strictly to direct, human-in-the-loop operational workflows** (e.g., manual ticket triage, live penetration testing, production IAM provisioning, manual firewall rule changes).
* **Automated CI/CD Exemption:** Automated pipelines, static security scanners, automated vulnerability remediation, and continuous delivery systems operate without staffing ratio constraints once their baseline security policies are stamped by a Master of Record.

## 4. Supervision Integrity & Enforcement Rules

* **Virtual Line-of-Sight & Distributed Supervision:** In distributed, remote, multi-cloud, or cross-border operations, direct supervision does not require physical co-location. Virtual line-of-sight is legally established through shared operational dashboards, ticket queue visibility, paired communication channels, and strict adherence to the **15-minute Active Reachability SLA**.
* **Prohibition of Solo Apprentice Shifts:** An apprentice may never be scheduled as the sole operator on a graveyard, weekend, or on-call rotation. Every active shift requires at least one designated on-duty Journeyman.
* **Active Reachability SLA:** Supervising Journeymen must be on active duty and directly reachable via real-time communication channels within a **15-minute response window** during the apprentice's shift.
* **Emergency Surge Incident Protocols:** During live P1 incident response crunches or urgent containment workflows, staffing ratios govern shift scheduling and designated supervision assignments; they do not create operational deadlocks. Paired apprentices may execute assigned triage and remediation tasks under the virtual supervision of the incident commander or lead Journeyman without requiring serial pre-approvals for standard containment playbooks.
* **Audit & Compliance Verification:** Adherence to supervisory ratios is logged via shift rosters and cryptographically attested through the **Actuarial Attestation Feed**. Willful violation of supervisory ratios constitutes an operational safety breach, subjecting the employer or contracting entity to immediate loss of preferred insurance discount tiers and potential board disciplinary review.

## 5. Operational Fatigue Mitigation, Shift Ceilings & Mandatory Rest Cycles

To eliminate vigilance degradation, burnout-induced detection misses, and dangerous cognitive fatigue during high-stress defensive operations:

* **The 14-Hour Incident Operational Ceiling:** No practitioner (Apprentice, Journeyman, or Master) may be compelled or permitted to perform active emergency incident response, forensic triage, or live SOC queue monitoring for more than **14 consecutive hours**.
* **Mandatory 10-Hour Uninterrupted Rest Cycle:** Any emergency shift or surge engagement extending past 12 consecutive hours mandates a minimum **10-hour uninterrupted recovery rest period** before the practitioner may be scheduled for active duty or on-call standby.
* **SOC Vigilance Cap (4-Hour Queue Limit):** In high-volume 24/7 Security Operations Centers, continuous live alert queue triage is capped at a maximum of **4 consecutive hours per shift**. For the remainder of the shift, the practitioner must rotate into proactive tasks (e.g., threat hunting, SIEM detection engineering, Python/Bash automation, documentation, or RTI lab training).
* **Solo On-Call Standby Ban:** Registered Apprentices are strictly prohibited from carrying primary pager duty or solo on-call incident response escalation rotations. All apprentice on-call learning must be secondary shadowing paired with an active, compensated Journeyman.

## 6. Dynamic Market Tapering & Counter-Cyclical Absorption

To prevent entry-level labor exploitation and avoid creating an unhirable surplus of newly minted Journeymen during macroeconomic contractions:

* **Trailing 12-Month Unemployment Indexing:** The National Board and regional JATC trusts monitor regional Journeyman unemployment using Guild Clearinghouse telemetry.
* **Apprentice Intake Tapering:** If regional Journeyman unemployment exceeds **5.0%**, private-sector apprentice intake quotas automatically taper downward to prioritize senior workforce absorption.
* **Intake Rebalance Threshold (8.0%):** If regional Journeyman unemployment exceeds **8.0%**, new private-sector indentures pause, and employer ratio caps rebalance to incentivize Journeyman retention.
* **The Counter-Cyclical Cyber Civil Defense Reserve (CCDR):** Rather than freezing training pipelines and locking out pre-apprentices during recessions, unabsorbed apprentices and candidates rotate into the **Cyber Civil Defense Reserve (CCDR)**. Trainees are dispatched to defend under-resourced public critical infrastructure (school districts, rural hospitals, municipal water utilities) funded through state/federal workforce resilience grants (e.g., WIOA Title I, CISA state resilience grants). This maintains training continuity, preserves the apprentice wage floor, and prevents post-recession talent vacuums.
* **Public Market Attrition Clawback & Retention Mandate:** To prevent municipal and public entities from exploiting the CCDR as a permanent source of free, subsidized labor to avoid budgeting for full Journeyman salaries:
  * **Supervisory Staffing Prerequisite:** Any public entity hosting CCDR apprentices must maintain at least one permanent, locally funded supervising Journeyman or retain a designated Trade Contractor.
  * **Mandatory Cohort Conversion Covenant:** A public entity utilizing CCDR apprentice cohorts must agree to convert at least one completing apprentice into a permanent, locally budgeted Journeyman position per 24-month cycle, or face a **36-month exclusion** from subsequent CCDR trainee allocations.
