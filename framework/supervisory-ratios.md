# Supervisory Ratios & Operational Headcount Standards

Supervisory ratios ensure quality mentorship, system stability, and fair labor distribution across both enterprise teams and managed service providers.

---

## 1. Standard Ratio Table

| Environment Type | Maximum Ratio (Apprentice : Journeyman) | Minimum Master Practitioner Oversight |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Enterprise SOC / IR** | **2 : 1** (per shift) | 1 Master per 8 Operational Staff |
| **IAM / Cloud Provisioning** | **2 : 1** | 1 Master per 6 Operational Staff |
| **Application Security / DevSecOps** | **3 : 1** | 1 Master per 10 Engineers |
| **Safety-Critical / IoMT / Medical** | **1 : 1** (strict line-of-sight) | 1 Master of Record mandatory |
| **Third-Party MSSP / Managed Services** | **2 : 1** (per client tenant tier) | Mandatory licensed oversight on all shifts |

---

## 2. Enforcement Rules
* **No Phantom Supervision:** Supervising Journeymen must be on active duty and directly reachable during the apprentice's operating window.
* **MSSP Tenant Caps:** In multi-tenant managed environments, a single Journeyman cannot supervise apprentices across more than 5 concurrent active client feeds simultaneously.
