# Supervisory Ratios & Operational Headcount Standards

Supervisory ratios ensure quality mentorship, system stability, operational safety, and sustainable workload distribution across enterprise teams, security operations centers (SOCs), and managed service providers (MSSPs).

---

## 1. Standard Supervisory Ratio Table

| Environment / Work Role | Max Ratio (Apprentice : Journeyman) | Minimum Master Practitioner Oversight | Scope & Setting |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Enterprise SOC / Incident Triage** | **2 : 1** (per operational shift) | 1 Master per 8 Operational Staff | Human-in-the-loop live alert queues |
| **IAM & Privileged Access Management** | **2 : 1** | 1 Master per 6 Operational Staff | Manual credential provisioning & role creation |
| **Penetration Testing & Red Teaming** | **2 : 1** | 1 Master per 6 Engineers | Active customer engagement scope |
| **AppSec & DevSecOps Engineering** | **3 : 1** | 1 Master per 10 Engineers | Threat modeling & code review workflows |
| **Safety-Critical / IoMT / Medical Systems** | **1 : 1** (strict direct oversight) | 1 Master of Record mandatory | Pre-market testing & clinical network access |
| **Industrial Control / OT / SCADA** | **1 : 1** (strict direct oversight) | 1 Master of Record mandatory | Live operational technology & safety networks |
| **MSSP / Managed Multi-Tenant Operations** | **2 : 1** (per client tenant tier) | Mandatory licensed oversight on all active shifts | Multi-tenant monitoring & customer triage |

---

## 2. Operational Boundaries & Velocity Exclusions

* **Human-in-the-Loop Scope Only:** Supervisory headcount ratios apply **strictly to direct, human-in-the-loop operational workflows** (e.g., manual ticket triage, live penetration testing, production IAM provisioning, manual firewall rule changes).
* **Automated CI/CD Exemption:** Automated pipelines, static security scanners, automated vulnerability remediation, and continuous delivery systems operate without staffing ratio constraints once their baseline security policies are stamped by a Master of Record.

---

## 3. Supervision Integrity & Enforcement Rules

* **Prohibition of Solo Apprentice Shifts:** An apprentice may never be scheduled as the sole operator on a graveyard, weekend, or on-call rotation. Every active shift requires at least one designated on-duty Journeyman.
* **Active Reachability SLA:** Supervising Journeymen must be on active duty and directly reachable via real-time communication channels within a **15-minute response window** during the apprentice's shift.
* **MSSP Tenant Throttling:** In multi-tenant environments, a single supervising Journeyman may not supervise apprentices across more than **5 concurrent active customer feeds** simultaneously, preventing diluted coverage.
* **Audit & Compliance Verification:** Adherence to supervisory ratios is logged via shift rosters and audited during periodic underwriter reviews and Board compliance inspections. Willful violation of supervisory ratios constitutes an operational safety breach, subjecting the employer to loss of preferred insurance discount tiers.
