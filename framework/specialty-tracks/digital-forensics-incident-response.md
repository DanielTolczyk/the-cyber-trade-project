## 1. Scope, Open Standards & NIST NICE Mapping

Digital Forensics & Incident Response (`SE-DFIR`) governs the rapid containment of cyber breaches, forensic artifact acquisition, malware reverse engineering, threat actor eradication, and the production of court-admissible evidence and statutory breach declarations.

Because DFIR investigations directly impact judicial proceedings, executive legal exposure, multi-million dollar cyber insurance claims, and statutory reporting obligations (e.g., SEC Item 1.05 Form 8-K, HIPAA, GDPR, CIRCIA), practitioners operate under strict chains of custody, verifiable hash integrity, and tamper-evident evidentiary protocols.

### A. Mandatory NIST NICE Framework Alignment (NIST SP 800-181)
Practitioners in this track demonstrate verified competencies mapped directly to federal work roles:
* **Primary Work Role 1: Cyber Defense Forensics Analyst (`IN-FOR-001`):** Executing bit-stream disk acquisition, volatile memory capture, filesystem timeline analysis, and artifact extraction.
* **Primary Work Role 2: Cyber Defense Incident Responder (`PR-CDA-001`):** Coordinating live incident containment, adversary eradication, lateral movement tracking, and enterprise network isolation.
* **Primary Work Role 3: Threat / Malware Analyst (`IN-FOR-002`):** Dissecting compiled binaries, decompiling malicious scripts, extracting indicators of compromise (IOCs), and analyzing command-and-control (C2) protocols.

### B. Authoritative Forensic Standards & Legal Baselines
1. **ISO/IEC 27037:** Guidelines for identification, collection, acquisition, and preservation of digital evidence.
2. **ISO/IEC 27042:** Guidelines for analysis and interpretation of digital evidence.
3. **NIST SP 800-86:** Guide to Integrating Forensic Techniques into Incident Response.
4. **NIST SP 800-61r2 / r3:** Computer Security Incident Handling Guide.
5. **RFC 3227:** Guidelines for Evidence Collection and Archiving (order of volatility).
6. **Federal Rules of Evidence (FRE Rule 902(13)/(14)):** Certified records generated by an electronic process or system and data copied from an electronic device.

## 2. The 2,000-Hour Specialized OJT Runtime Breakdown

To earn the `SE-DFIR` Endorsement, a Licensed Journeyman must complete 2,000 verified post-licensure on-the-job operational hours under the line-of-sight supervision of an active `SE-DFIR` Master Practitioner across four specialized domains:

```
+─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────+
|               SE-DFIR: 2,000-HOUR SPECIALTY RUNTIME DISTRIBUTION            |
+─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────+
  ├─► Domain 1: Evidentiary Triage & Chain of Custody           ──► 500 Hours
  ├─► Domain 2: Disk, Memory & Cloud Artifact Reconstruction    ──► 500 Hours
  ├─► Domain 3: Incident Containment & Lateral Movement Tracing ──► 600 Hours
  └─► Domain 4: Statutory Breach Attestations & Proof of Loss   ──► 400 Hours
+─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────+
```

### Domain 1: Evidentiary Triage & Chain of Custody (500 Hours)
* Performing write-blocked physical and logical disk acquisitions, volatile RAM captures adhering to RFC 3227 order of volatility, and cloud storage snapshots.
* Generating cryptographic SHA-256 / SHA-512 hashes and maintaining immutable physical and digital Chain of Custody logs.
* Establishing secure, segmented forensic analysis enclaves to prevent cross-contamination or accidental evidence spoliation.


### Domain 2: Disk, Memory & Cloud Artifact Reconstruction (500 Hours)
* Analyzing file systems (NTFS, APFS, EXT4) for Master File Table ($MFT) entries, USN journals, shellbags, shimcache, and unallocated space artifacts.
* Conducting volatile memory analysis to identify unlinked processes, code injection, reflective DLL loading, and dumped credential structures.
* Extracting and correlating cloud telemetry (AWS CloudTrail, Azure Activity Logs, Google Cloud Audit, Microsoft 365 Unified Audit Logs) to reconstruct multi-tenant intrusion paths.

### Domain 3: Incident Containment & Lateral Movement Tracing (600 Hours)
* Reconstructing comprehensive intrusion timelines and kill chains mapped to the MITRE ATT&CK framework.
* Tracking adversary lateral movement across Kerberos tickets (Pass-the-Ticket, Overpass-the-Hash), WMI, PowerShell remoting, and RDP sessions.
* Engineering surgical containment actions (host isolation, credential revocation, session invalidation, rogue infrastructure sinkholing) to stop data exfiltration without tipping off advanced threat actors.
* Validating complete adversary eradication prior to enterprise system recovery.

### Domain 4: Statutory Breach Attestations & Proof of Loss (400 Hours)
* Authoring technical root-cause analysis (RCA) reports, forensic chronologies, and executive impact summaries.
* Compiling formal digital evidence packages conforming to court admissibility standards and insurer proof-of-loss requirements.
* Performing statutory impact scoping to determine the exact volume, categorization, and exfiltration status of regulated data (PII, PHI, NPI, export-controlled technical data).

## 3. Master of Record (MoR) Authority for DFIR

A Master Practitioner holding the `SE-DFIR` Endorsement possesses statutory sign-off authority for:

1. **Evidentiary Chain of Custody Certification:** Digitally signing forensic evidence packages, verifying hash integrity and acquisition methodology under Federal Rules of Evidence baselines.
2. **Insurer Proof-of-Loss Containment Attestation:** Certifying to cyber underwriting syndicates and actuarial bodies that an active breach has been contained, threat actors eradicated, and persistence mechanisms neutralized.
3. **Statutory Breach Scoping Sign-Off:** Formally certifying the technical scope, data exfiltration boundaries, and root-cause determination for public regulatory disclosures and notification filings.
4. **Notice of Safety Non-Concurrence Standing:** Authority to issue a formal [Notice of Safety Non-Concurrence (Form FORM-001)](../../templates/FORM-001_notice-of-safety-non-concurrence.md) if executive leadership orders premature evidence destruction, concealment of material breach findings, or unsanitized recovery before threat eradication.

---

## 4. Strict Cross-Crediting & Articulation Boundaries

Forensic investigation is an evidentiary discipline requiring strict legal and analytical integrity:

* **Zero Cross-Crediting from Software / AppSec Tracks:** `SE-APP` provides **0 hours of cross-credit** toward `SE-DFIR`. Software development and SAST tuning do not satisfy evidentiary chain of custody or live incident containment requirements.
* **Zero Cross-Crediting with Industrial Controls (`SE-ICS`):** `SE-ICS` and `SE-DFIR` do not cross-credit.
* **General Apprenticeship Foundations:** Rotations in Security Operations / Incident Handling during the 8,000-hour core apprenticeship provide the prerequisite qualifying baseline, but cannot reduce the required 2,000 post-licensure `SE-DFIR` runtime hours.
