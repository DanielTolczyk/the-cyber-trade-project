# Code of Ethics & Formal Refusal Protocol

The trade framework empowers practitioners to prioritize public safety, data integrity, and compliance over corporate convenience.

---

## 1. Core Ethical Tenets
1. **Protection of Public Trust & Life-Safety:** Prioritize the security and physical safety of users and critical infrastructure above commercial expedience.
2. **Duty of Candor:** Provide complete, unvarnished risk assessments to leadership without obscuring known vulnerabilities.
3. **Refusal of Substandard Systems:** Refuse to deploy or sign off on systems known to be in violation of baseline security standards.
4. **Continuous Competence:** Maintain active mastery of evolving threat landscapes, defensive engineering, and regulatory standards.

---

## 2. The Formal Refusal Workflow

1. **Unsafe Directive Issued:** An instruction is received to bypass controls, push non-compliant code, or deploy unsafe infrastructure.
2. **Informal Technical Consultation:** Practitioner presents technical risk analysis and offers a compliant remediation path.
3. **Formal Notice of Non-Concurrence:** If the directive stands, the licensed engineer files an official written Notice of Non-Concurrence with copies to the Authorizing Officer, CISO, and Corporate Compliance, logged permanently in the enterprise risk register.
4. **Resolution Path:**
   * **Remediation:** Leadership adopts the remediation path -> Sign-off proceeds.
   * **Executive Risk Assumption:** Executive officer executes formal written Risk Acceptance -> Legal liability shifts entirely from the engineer to the corporate officer.
