# Code of Ethics, Civil Rights & Professional Conduct

Professional accountability requires that licensed practitioners and candidates possess clear ethical standards, robust civil rights protections, and an enforceable code of conduct. This specification establishes the **Ethical Canons of the Trade**, explicit protections against discrimination and harassment across all protected classes, disciplinary violation categories, the **Mandatory Reporting Affirmative Duty**, the investigative division of authority, and the **Four-Tier Disciplinary Sanction Matrix**.

---

## 1. Ethical Canons of the Trade

Every registered Pre-Apprentice candidate, Apprentice, Journeyman, and Master Practitioner is bound by six foundational canons:

1. **Primacy of Public Safety & System Integrity:** Safeguard human safety, critical infrastructure, and data integrity above commercial expedience, speed-to-market pressures, or administrative convenience.
2. **Objective Engineering Candor:** Provide unvarnished, mathematically sound, and auditable assessments of risk, vulnerabilities, architectural deficiencies, and defensive readiness.
3. **Guardianship of Competency & Truthful Telemetry:** Never falsify logbook hours, forge supervisory signatures, sign off on unverified operational artifacts, or practice beyond accredited domain competencies.
4. **Universal Civil Rights, Non-Discrimination & Mutual Respect:** Uphold equal opportunity, dignity, and inclusive safety across all educational, dispatch, and operational settings. Never engage in or tolerate harassment, discrimination, or hate speech.
5. **Protection of Whistleblowers & Affirmative Mandatory Reporting:** Actively report verified ethical violations, logbook fraud, and civil rights infractions. Defend peers and subordinates who invoke ethical escalation procedures in good faith.
6. **Prohibition of Supervisory Coercion & Exploitation:** Never condition subordinate career progression, logbook attestations, wage advancement, or training evaluations on personal, political, or organizational compliance. Senior practitioners must actively defend the independent agency and welfare of the trainees under their supervision.

## 2. Protected Classes & Anti-Discrimination Mandate

The cybersecurity trade enforces strict non-discrimination protections anchored in federal labor and civil rights statutes, including Title VII of the Civil Rights Act of 1964, the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA), the Age Discrimination in Employment Act (ADEA), USERRA, the Pregnant Workers Fairness Act (PWFA), the CROWN Act, and Department of Labor Apprenticeship Equal Employment Opportunity regulations (29 CFR Part 30).

Discrimination, disparate treatment, or harassment based on any of the following protected classes is strictly prohibited across all JATC training centers, practical challenge examination environments, employer rotations, and Guild union halls:

* **Race, Color, and National Origin:** Full protection against race-based bias, national origin discrimination, or linguistic profiling across candidate screening, rotational assignments, and wage step advancement.
* **Sex, Gender Identity, and Sexual Orientation:** Comprehensive protections (incorporating *Bostock v. Clayton County* and the Equal Pay Act) prohibiting disparate treatment, deadnaming, gender-based wage disparities, or biased evaluation rubrics.
* **Religion & Sincere Belief:** Mandatory reasonable accommodation for religious observances, dietary requirements, and scheduled Sabbath or prayer cycles during operational shifts and rotational schedules.
* **Disability & Neurodivergence:** Mandatory reasonable accommodations under ADA Title I and Rehabilitation Act Sections 503/504 for physical, sensory, and neurodivergent accessibility across cyber ranges, SOC consoles, instructional platforms, and exam environments.
* **Veteran & Active Military Status:** USERRA and VEVRAA protections guaranteeing re-entry into active apprentice rotations, credit for military cyber operational experience, and protection of benefit hour-banks during deployment.
* **Age (40 and Older):** Prohibition against age-based career barriers under ADEA, supporting mid-career transitioners into trade apprenticeships.
* **Pregnancy, Childbirth & Related Conditions:** Comprehensive protections under PWFA and Title VII for pregnancy-related accommodations, lactation access, parental leave, and pause-and-resume logbook protections without loss of standing.
* **Genetic Information:** Absolute prohibition under GINA against collecting or utilizing genetic data or family medical histories in benefit trusts or pre-employment evaluations.
* **Citizenship & Immigration Status:** Protection for all legally authorized workers under INA Section 274B, with explicit exemptions strictly restricted to roles where federal statutory security clearances (ITAR/FedRAMP/Cleared Defense) mandate specific citizenship.
* **Natural Hair & Cultural Expression:** Adherence to CROWN Act standards prohibiting discriminatory grooming or presentation policies.
* **Concerted Labor Activity:** Absolute protection under the National Labor Relations Act (NLRA) for participating in Guild governance, reporting grievances, or organizing trade labor.

## 3. Disciplinary Violation Classifications

Ethical infractions and misconduct are categorized into four distinct classes:

### Class A: Discrimination, Harassment & Hate Speech
* **Quid Pro Quo Harassment:** Conditioning logbook attestations, wage step elevations, rotational assignments, or exam endorsements on sexual favors, personal subservience, or ideological conformity.
* **Hostile Work Environment:** Pervasive or severe derogatory slurs, discriminatory behavior, unwanted physical contact, targeted mockery, or exclusionary practices based on any protected class.
* **Hate Speech & Targeted Intimidation:** Use of slurs, hate group iconography, threats of violence, or digital harassment across corporate communication platforms, JATC classrooms, code commits, or dispatch halls.

### Class B: Operational Integrity & Telemetry Falsification
* **Logbook Fraud:** Deliberate falsification, inflation, or fabrication of operational runtime hours, incident tickets, change hashes, or range lab credits.
* **Supervisory Extortion & Malicious Withholding:** A Journeyman or Master conditioning legitimate runtime sign-offs on non-work favors, or maliciously refusing to sign verified hours to stall an apprentice's progression.
* **Fraudulent Attestation & CVE Concealment:** Signing off on unverified security controls, concealing known critical vulnerabilities, or submitting fraudulent audit filings to insurance carriers or regulatory bodies.
* **Academic Dishonesty:** Cheating on pre-apprenticeship exit exams or Board practical challenge evaluations through unauthorized braindumps, answer sharing, or proxy test-taking.

### Class C: Retaliation, Whistleblower Suppression & Deanonymization
* **Direct Retaliation:** Terminating, demoting, docking wages, or giving unwarranted negative reviews to any practitioner who files Form FORM-001, FORM-003, or FORM-004.
* **Proxy Retaliation & Social Ostracization:** Peers, colleagues, or friends retaliating on behalf of a disciplined individual, including deliberate cold-shouldering, refusing to pair-program, stalling Pull Request (PR) reviews, or withholding assistance during live operational incidents and on-call rotations.
* **Deanonymization Attacks & Interrogations:** Interrogating coworkers, reviewing confidential logs, or conducting inquiries aimed at identifying or unmasking confidential whistleblowers, reporting parties, or participating witnesses.
* **Immigration & Sponsorship Coercion:** Threatening to revoke, delay, or withhold visa sponsorships, work authorizations, or apprentice indentures as leverage against reporting or refusal.
* **Blacklisting:** Organizing, circulating, or participating in corporate, local chapter, or informal industry blacklists intended to restrict a licensed practitioner's employment, contracting, or trade advancement.

### Class D: Supervisory Abuse, Ratio Exploitation & Algorithmic Coercion
* Assigning apprentices to non-defensive tasks (e.g., personal errands, uncompensated clerical labor) unrelated to accredited trade domains.
* Violating the mandatory 2:1 on-shift operational supervisory ratio, abandoning trainees on solo unmonitored shifts during high-risk production incidents or graveyard rotations.
* Utilizing automated AI scoring algorithms, bossware telemetry, or surveillance quotas to terminate, discipline, or demote practitioners without human supervisory review.
* Dereliction of the affirmative mandatory reporting duty upon witnessing verified Class A, B, or C violations.

## 4. Mandatory Reporting Affirmative Duty, Blind Proxy & Witness Protection

Cybersecurity is an operational discipline grounded in verifiable integrity. A culture of silence or peer intimidation is incompatible with public safety and system defense.

### A. Universal Affirmative Duty
* Every registered Apprentice, Licensed Journeyman, and Master Practitioner who possesses direct, verifiable knowledge of a Class A, Class B, or Class C violation is required to file an [Ethics & Conduct Incident Report (Form FORM-004)](../templates/FORM-004_ethics-and-conduct-incident-report.md) within ten (10) business days of discovery.
* **Supervisory Heightened Standard:** Journeymen, Masters of Record, and JATC Instructors who observe harassment, discrimination, or logbook fraud among personnel under their direct supervision must submit Form FORM-004 within 48 hours.
* **Willful Concealment Sanction:** Active concealment, destruction of telemetry evidence, or conspiracy to hide verified violations constitutes a secondary ethical infraction subject to formal Board censure.

### B. Blind Whistleblower Proxy Protocols (The Ombudsperson Institutional Shield)
To protect vulnerable practitioners from social ostracization or career retaliation when reporting influential or popular individuals:
* **The Ombuds Blind Proxy:** A reporting party may request that the **Guild Ombudsperson** serve as their institutional proxy. The Ombudsperson reviews the underlying evidence, verifies witness statements, and formally enters the complaint into the docket under the Ombudsperson's official standing.
* **Total Identity Sealing:** The reporting party's personal identity is permanently sealed in confidential executive trust records and is never disclosed on the public Board docket, to the accused party, or to corporate management.

### C. Universal Participant & Witness Safe Harbor
* **Comprehensive Protection:** Every individual who provides witness statements, telemetry data, code artifacts, or interview testimony during an ethics investigation receives the identical statutory Whistleblower Shield and Guild Legal Defense indemnification granted to primary filers.
* **Strict Anti-Retaliation Immunity:** Any adverse action taken against a participating witness within 12 months is treated as an aggravated Class C violation.

## 5. Investigative Structure & Division of Authority

Investigations are handled by distinct, independent entities based on the nature of the alleged violation:

| Investigating Body | Composition | Jurisdiction & Scope | Authority & Sanctions |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Board Malpractice & Ethics Review Committee** | Tripartite panel (Master Practitioner, Employer CISO, Public Safety Legal Advocate) | Professional licensure integrity, logbook fraud, false compliance attestations, statutory refusal retaliation. | Public censure, license suspension/revocation, MoR debarment, clearinghouse de-registration. |
| **Guild Member Welfare & Civil Rights Committee** | Elected Guild Ombudsperson, 2 active Journeymen, 1 Apprentice Delegate | Harassment, discrimination, hate speech, toxic workplace grievances, worker advocacy. | Emergency apprentice reassignment, Guild membership suspension, referral to Board for license review. |
| **JATC Apprenticeship Disciplinary Panel** | Equal representation of Guild Training Directors and Employer Trustees | Training trust compliance, 2:1 ratio violations, wage step withholding, pre-apprentice cheating. | Wage restitution enforcement, supervisor sign-off remediation, training sponsor debarment. |
| **Independent External Investigator** | Retained independent employment/civil rights attorney with no financial ties to parties | High-conflict complaints involving Board Trustees, C-suite executives, or systemic civil rights claims. | Comprehensive fact-finding report submitted directly to the full 11-member Board. |

### A. Mandatory Interim Protective Orders (Active Investigation Safe Harbor)
To prevent intimidation or subtle harassment while a formal inquiry is underway (14 to 30 days):
* **Immediate Supervisory Firewall:** The accused supervisor is immediately relieved of direct evaluation, performance rating, shift scheduling, and logbook sign-off authority over the reporting party and participating witnesses.
* **Mutual No-Contact Directive:** Investigators issue a binding administrative directive barring the accused from communicating directly or indirectly with the filer and witnesses outside formal investigative hearings.
* **Non-Prejudicial Work Adjustments:** Sponsoring employers must accommodate remote shift rotations, alternative pairing, or temporary JATC lab reassignment at full pay without penalizing the reporting practitioner.

### Institutional & Employer Sanctions:
* **Emergency Apprentice Reassignment:** The JATC immediately pulls indentured apprentices from non-compliant employers and reassigns them with zero loss of wage step or accredited hours.

* **Council Debarment:** Sponsoring employer status is suspended or terminated by the Participating Employer Council.

## 7. Post-Investigation Monitoring & The 12-Month Retaliation Presumption

Retaliation often occurs months after an inquiry concludes, manifesting as subtle career friction, stalled promotions, or negative performance reviews:

* **The 12-Month Monitoring Window:** Following any formal finding or reprimand, the Guild Member Welfare Committee places the reporting party's operational environment under an active 12-month monitoring cycle.
* **Quarterly Telemetry & Review Audits:** The Guild conducts quarterly confidential reviews of the practitioner's PR review turnaround times, incident shift distribution, on-call standby allocations, and logbook attestation velocity.
* **Rebuttable Presumption of Retaliation:** Any adverse employment action (demotion, termination, negative evaluation, refusal of wage step, or disciplinary write-up) taken against a whistleblower or participating witness within 365 days of a case closing carries a **legal and administrative presumption of retaliation**. The employer or supervisor bears the full burden of proving through clear, objective technical telemetry that the adverse action was entirely unrelated to the prior report.
