# Specialty Endorsements & Domain Architecture

Cybersecurity is not a monolith. Following the completion of core generalist rotations (Years 1–2), practitioners pursue specific trade endorsements that validate deep technical and regulatory expertise.

---

## 1. Core Generalist Rotation (Mandatory for all Tiers 1–2)
Before specializing, all practitioners must log validated hours across four foundational pillars:
1. **Network Defense & Protocols:** Packet inspection, firewalling, DNS/routing security.
2. **Identity & Access Management (IAM):** Active Directory, PAM, OAuth/SAML, principle of least privilege.
3. **Endpoint & Systems Hardening:** Linux/Windows internals, baseline configurations, patch management.
4. **Security Operations & Triage:** Incident classification, log analysis, baseline threat hunting.

---

## 2. Specialized Trade Branches (Tier 3 through Master)

### Branch A: Risk Management, Threat Modeling & Regulatory Compliance
* **Core Focus:** Threat modeling (STRIDE, PASTA), risk assessments (NIST RMF, ISO 27005), security requirements authoring, regulatory compliance.
* **Recognized Certifications / Equivalencies:** CRISC, Open FAIR, CISM, sector-specific regulatory accreditations.
* **Sign-off Authority:** Formal risk acceptance memos, regulatory product submissions (e.g., FDA pre-market cybersecurity documentation), third-party risk attestations.

### Branch B: Defensive Operations & Incident Response
* **Core Focus:** Advanced digital forensics, malware reverse engineering, threat hunting, active containment.
* **Recognized Certifications / Equivalencies:** GCFA, GCIH, BTL2, GIAC Certified Incident Handler.
* **Sign-off Authority:** Breach containment verification, incident post-mortem filings to insurance underwriters and regulators.

### Branch C: Offensive Security & System Assurance
* **Core Focus:** Penetration testing, red teaming, vulnerability research, source code audits.
* **Recognized Certifications / Equivalencies:** OSCP, OSEP, GXPN, CREST.
* **Sign-off Authority:** Formal penetration test sign-offs, attestation of external attack surface resilience.

### Branch D: Product & Medical Device Security (IoMT / Embedded)
* **Core Focus:** Firmware hardening, hardware security, secure boot, threat modeling for safety-critical systems, FDA 524B compliance.
* **Sign-off Authority:** Safety-critical cybersecurity readiness for product release.
