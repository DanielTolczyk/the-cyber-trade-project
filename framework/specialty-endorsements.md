# Specialty Endorsements & Domain Architecture

Cybersecurity is not a monolith. Following the completion of core generalist rotations (Years 1–2), practitioners pursue specific trade endorsements that validate deep technical, legal, and operational expertise.

---

## 1. Core Generalist Rotation (Mandatory for all Tiers 1–2)
Before specializing, all practitioners must log validated hours across four foundational pillars:
1. **Network Defense & Protocols:** Packet inspection, firewalling, DNS/routing security.
2. **Identity & Access Management (IAM):** Active Directory, PAM, OAuth/SAML, principle of least privilege.
3. **Endpoint & Systems Hardening:** Linux/Windows internals, baseline configurations, patch management.
4. **Security Operations & Triage:** Incident classification, log analysis, baseline threat hunting.

---

## 2. Specialized Trade Branches (Tier 3 through Master)

### Branch A: Digital Forensics & Incident Response (DFIR)
* **Trade Parallel:** Emergency Hazardous Material (HAZMAT) & Disaster Containment.
* **Core Focus:** Active containment, disk/memory forensics, chain-of-custody evidence preservation, malware reverse engineering, regulatory incident timeline reconstruction.
* **Recognized Credentials:** GCFA, GCFE, GNFA, GCIH.
* **Sign-off Authority:** Sworn evidentiary chain of custody, formal breach containment verification for insurance claims, SEC/regulatory incident attestation.

### Branch B: Cyber Risk Management, Threat Modeling & Compliance (GRC)
* **Trade Parallel:** Structural Plan Examiner & Building Code Inspector.
* **Core Focus:** Threat modeling (STRIDE, PASTA), risk quantification (Open FAIR), security requirements authoring, NIST RMF/ISO governance.
* **Recognized Credentials:** CRISC, Open FAIR, CISM.
* **Sign-off Authority:** Formal risk acceptance attestations, regulatory compliance filings, third-party vendor risk certifications.

### Branch C: Offensive Security & Adversary Emulation
* **Trade Parallel:** Physical Penetration & Stress Testing Specialist.
* **Core Focus:** Network/web penetration testing, red teaming, exploit development, social engineering resilience, MITRE ATT&CK adversary emulation.
* **Recognized Credentials:** OSCP, OSEP, GXPN, CREST.
* **Sign-off Authority:** Penetration test certification, external attack surface resilience sign-off.

### Branch D: Medical Device & IoMT Security
* **Trade Parallel:** Biomedical Systems Specialist.
* **Core Focus:** Embedded device security, FDA 524B compliance, medical device threat modeling, secure boot, clinical network integration.
* **Recognized Credentials:** Sector-specific biomedical/IoMT credentials.
* **Sign-off Authority:** Pre-market FDA cybersecurity readiness sign-off, clinical product release authorization.

### Branch E: Industrial Control Systems (ICS / SCADA / OT)
* **Trade Parallel:** Master Power Plant / High-Voltage Lineman.
* **Core Focus:** Purdue Model segmentation, SCADA protocols (Modbus, DNP3), safety instrumented systems (SIS), IEC 62443, NERC-CIP compliance.
* **Recognized Credentials:** GICSP, GRID, GCIP.
* **Sign-off Authority:** Industrial safety cyber clearance, air-gapped control system maintenance sign-off.

### Branch F: Identity, Credential & Access Management (ICAM)
* **Trade Parallel:** High-Security Master Locksmith & Access Barrier Engineer.
* **Core Focus:** Zero Trust architecture, enterprise federation (SAML/OIDC), Privileged Access Management (PAM), secrets management lifecycle.
* **Recognized Credentials:** CIAM, specialized cloud identity credentials.
* **Sign-off Authority:** Enterprise federation sign-off, emergency root credential rotation attestation.

### Branch G: Cryptographic Infrastructure & PKI
* **Trade Parallel:** Vault Security & Cryptographic Architect.
* **Core Focus:** Hardware Security Module (HSM) deployment, enterprise PKI lifecycle, root CA isolation, Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC) migration.
* **Recognized Credentials:** Board-approved cryptography specializations.
* **Sign-off Authority:** Root CA key ceremony execution and witness sign-off, cryptographic protocol modernization approval.
