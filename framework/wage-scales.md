# Standardized Wage Floors & Compensation Framework

The trade wage framework establishes a **guaranteed legal and operational floor**, not a ceiling. It prevents the exploitation of entry-level practitioners and sets clear minimum standards while preserving uncapped upside, bonuses, equity, and over-scale compensation for specialized talent.

---

## 1. Baseline Progression Scale (The Floor)
Percentages are anchored to the local or national **Journeyman Prevailing Base Floor** (e.g., $110,000 – $130,000 baseline depending on COLI).

| Level | Milestone Requirements | Minimum Wage Floor (% of Journeyman Base) | Scope of Authority |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Apprentice Tier 1** | Pre-Apprenticeship Exit / Onboarding | **50%** | Foundational triage, basic systems hygiene, supervised provisioning. |
| **Apprentice Tier 2** | 2,000 Hours + Core Domain Rotation | **65%** | Network defense, identity management, vulnerability scanning. |
| **Apprentice Tier 3** | 4,000 Hours + Track Declaration | **75%** | Junior penetration testing, risk assessments, threat model drafting. |
| **Apprentice Tier 4** | 6,000 Hours + Advanced Rotation | **85%** | Incident handling, tool deployment, requirement specification. |
| **Journeyman** | 8,000 Hours + CISSP / Board Exam | **100% (Baseline Floor)** | Independent production changes, peer sign-off, apprentice supervision. |
| **Master / Principal** | 12,000+ Hours + Specialty Authority | **130% – 160%+ Floor** | Enterprise threat modeling sign-off, regulatory filings, liability authority. |

---

## 2. Sector & Hazard Differentials (High-Consequence Premiums)
Specialized sectors involving human safety, critical infrastructure, and strict regulatory compliance mandate minimum percentage differentials above the base rate:

* **Life-Safety & Medical Devices (FDA / IoMT):** +20% to +35% minimum differential.
* **Industrial Control Systems / OT (SCADA, NERC-CIP):** +20% to +30% minimum differential.
* **National Security / Defense Systems:** +25% minimum differential.
* **Offensive Exploitation & Zero-Day Research:** +25% to +40% minimum differential.

---

## 3. Market Flexibility & Incentives
* **Over-Scale Compensation:** Employers are fully permitted to pay above standard trade minimums to secure high-demand talent.
* **Performance Incentives:** Bonuses, profit sharing, and equity structures remain independent of the wage floor.
* **Portability:** If an engineer moves between employers, their recognized baseline tier and logged hours guarantee they cannot be down-leveled below their earned trade floor.
