# Licensure Standards, Progression Milestones & Tier Elevation

This specification defines the normative operational requirements governing career progression, milestone evaluations, and professional licensure across all tiers of the cybersecurity trade.

---

## 1. Overview of the Licensure Hierarchy

The trade enforces a unified, transparent progression model based on verified runtime hours, Related Technical Instruction (RTI), objective practical examination, and peer portfolio defense:

## 2. Apprentice Progression Tiers (Years 1 through 4)

Apprentices advance through four structured compensation and responsibility tiers. Advancement occurs automatically upon logging the required runtime hours and completing annual RTI modules:

| Tier | Runtime Hours | RTI Requirement | Wage Floor (% RJPB) | Primary Scope & Autonomy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1** | 0 - 2,000 hrs | 144 hrs (Year 1) | 50% | Foundational hygiene, guided triage, 1:1 supervision. |
| **Tier 2** | 2,001 - 4,000 hrs | 288 hrs (Year 2) | 60% | SOC triage, guided patching, pipeline scanning. |
| **Tier 3** | 4,001 - 6,000 hrs | 432 hrs (Year 3) | 70% | Threat hunting, automated script authoring, policy audits. |
| **Tier 4** | 6,001 - 8,000 hrs | 576 hrs (Year 4) | 80% | Advanced hardening, specialty electives, exam prep. |

## 3. The Journeyman Licensure Standard

To graduate from apprentice status and achieve independent Journeyman Licensure, a practitioner must satisfy four mandatory criteria:

1. **Verified Runtime Completion:** Complete 8,000 verified operational hours distributed across the 5 core rotational domains defined in [Apprenticeship Standards](apprenticeship-standards.md).
2. **Instructional Runtime:** Complete 576 hours of accredited Related Technical Instruction (RTI) through a JATC training trust or accredited community college.
3. **Logbook Audit:** Submit an auditable [Universal Logbook](logbook-standards.md) with cryptographic supervisor signatures for all logged milestones.
4. **Practical Board Examination:** Pass the proctored, hands-on National Cybersecurity Trade Board Practical Challenge Examination evaluating live threat containment, incident triage, and systems hardening under simulated operational conditions.

### Journeyman Operational Authority
* Practice independently without direct line-of-sight supervision.
* Execute production change tickets and deploy defensive configurations.
* Supervise up to two registered apprentices on an active shift (2:1 ratio).
* Issue internal [Operational Exception Flags (Form FORM-003)](../templates/FORM-003_operational-exception-flag.md).

## 4. Master Practitioner Elevation

Master Practitioner represents the highest individual technical credential in the trade. Elevation is open to Licensed Journeymen who demonstrate deep technical mastery and leadership:

1. **Runtime Prerequisite:** Complete a minimum of **12,000 verified operational runtime hours** (an additional 4,000 hours beyond Journeyman licensure).
2. **Specialty Domain Mastery:** Hold at least one accredited [Specialty Endorsement](specialty-endorsements.md) (e.g., ICS/SCADA, DFIR, Cloud Security, AI/ML Assurance).
3. **Defense Portfolio Audit:** Submit a comprehensive defense portfolio containing sanitized architectural blueprints, incident post-mortems, automation tools, or research publications.
4. **Oral Board Defense:** Successfully defend their portfolio before an evaluation panel of three active Master Practitioners convened by the Board.
5. **Ethical Record:** Maintain a clean ethical standing with zero unresolved malpractice findings or disciplinary suspensions.

---

## 5. Designated Master of Record (MoR) Assignment

A Master of Record is a Master Practitioner who accepts legal and statutory responsibility for an enterprise, critical system, or licensed trade contracting firm:

* **Board Registration:** The sponsoring employer or Trade Contractor registers the designated MoR with the National Board Registry.
* **Statutory Sign-Off Authority:** Holds the sole authority to inspect and stamp production security baselines, root cryptographic ceremonies, and regulatory filings.
* **Refusal & Non-Concurrence:** Holds exclusive legal standing to file a [Notice of Safety Non-Concurrence (Form FORM-001)](../templates/FORM-001_notice-of-safety-non-concurrence.md) when executive directives violate statutory safety baselines.

## 6. License Maintenance & Triennial Renewal

To eliminate predatory commercial recertification fees while guaranteeing active competence:

* **Zero-Fee Renewal:** The Board charges zero annual maintenance fees (AMFs) for credential renewal.
* **Triennial Audit Cycle:** Licenses are renewed every 3 years upon submitting proof of:
  * Minimum 1,000 verified operational runtime hours per year (or approved instructional/community technical contributions).
  * Completion of 40 hours per year of continuing technical development (CTD) or advanced lab coursework.
  * An updated ethics attestation affirming compliance with Trade Canons.
