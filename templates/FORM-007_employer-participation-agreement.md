## 1. Employer Covenants & Apprenticeship Sponsorship

The Sponsoring Employer hereby agrees to sponsor registered apprenticeship seats under the National Guidelines for Apprenticeship Standards registered with the U.S. Department of Labor (29 CFR Part 29). The Employer agrees to:

1. **Provide Progressive Operational Runtime:** Employ registered apprentices for a minimum of 2,000 operational hours per calendar year across the 5 core trade domains (Domain 1: Perimeter/Network, Domain 2: Detection/SOC, Domain 3: IAM, Domain 4: Vulnerability, Domain 5: Defensive GRC).
2. **Enforce Mandatory Supervisory Ratios:** Maintain an on-shift operational supervision ratio of no greater than **2 registered apprentices to 1 licensed Journeyman or Master Practitioner** on all active operational rosters.
3. **Comply with Wage Step Schedule:** Compensate registered apprentices in strict accordance with the mandatory progressive wage step schedule:
   * **Tier 1 Apprentice (0 - 2,000 hrs):** 50% Regional Journeyman Prevailing Base (RJPB)
   * **Tier 2 Apprentice (2,001 - 4,000 hrs):** 60% RJPB
   * **Tier 3 Apprentice (4,001 - 6,000 hrs):** 70% RJPB
   * **Tier 4 Apprentice (6,001 - 8,000 hrs):** 80% RJPB
   * **Licensed Journeyman (8,001+ hrs & Exam Pass):** 100% RJPB

## 2. Training Trust Remittance & Educational Release

1. **Hourly Trust Remittance:** Sponsoring Employer agrees to remit the mandatory JATC Training Trust contribution of `$[HOURLY_RATE]` per compensable trade hour directly to the JATC Trust Fund established under Form FORM-006.
2. **Paid Educational Release:** Employer shall release registered apprentices with pay for mandatory 144 hours per year of Related Technical Instruction (RTI) classroom and range lab training.
3. **Digital Logbook Compliance:** Sponsoring Employer agrees to verify and digitally sign apprentice runtime hours quarterly using the Universal Digital Logbook standard under recognized Modality A, B, C, or D protocols.

By maintaining active compliance with this Agreement, the Sponsoring Employer qualifies for:
1. **Pillar VII Preferred Premium Credits:** 25% to 35% cyber liability insurance warranty discounts through participating Cyber Underwriting & Actuarial Advisory Consortium (CUAAC) underwriters.
2. **Statutory Non-Concurrence Safe Harbor:** Legal safe harbor protections when implementing technical risk acceptance procedures under Form FORM-002.
