# Guild Formation, Labor Trusts & Structural Governance

This document establishes the roadmap for chartering the Cybersecurity Craft Guild, outlining its institutional separation from the Regulatory Board and the step-by-step formation of multi-employer bargaining units and training trusts.

---

## 1. Separation of Powers: Board vs. Guild

| Dimension | The National Licensure Board | The Cybersecurity Craft Guild |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Mandate** | Public safety, system integrity, and credential enforcement. | Worker protection, wage standards, legal defense, and training delivery. |
| **Institutional Model** | Non-profit regulatory body (modeled after State Licensing Boards / NCEES). | Professional craft association & labor trust (modeled after IBEW / JATC). |
| **Core Functions** | Administering exams, issuing licenses, investigating malpractice, revoking credentials. | Collective bargaining, administering JATC training trusts, providing legal defense for refusal. |
| **Relationship** | The Board sets the standards for licensure. | The Guild trains workers to meet Board standards and protects their rights in the field. |

---

## 2. Phased Roadmap for Guild Formation

### Phase 1: The Voluntary Professional Association (The Organizing Phase)
* **Structure:** Formed as a non-profit 501(c)(6) professional association or independent advocacy body.
* **Focus:** Enrolling founding Journeymen and Masters, establishing model employment contracts, and pooling resources for an initial Refusal Legal Defense Fund.
* **Enterprise Alignment:** Partnering with forward-leaning employers, community colleges, and cyber-insurance underwriters to launch pilot apprenticeship consortiums.

### Phase 2: Multi-Employer Training Trusts (JATC Formation)
* **Legal Architecture:** Establishing a multi-employer joint apprenticeship and training trust under Section 302(c) of the Labor Management Relations Act (Taft-Hartley Act).
* **Employer Contribution Agreements:** Participating employers (enterprises, MSSPs, government contractors) agree to contribute a fixed fee per billable cybersecurity labor hour into the Trust.
* **Educational Deployment:** The Trust funds community college lab environments, pays instructors, and provides $0-tuition instruction to registered apprentices.

### Phase 3: Formal Collective Bargaining Units & Regional Locals
* **Statutory Recognition:** Transitioning to recognized craft bargaining units representing security practitioners within enterprises, contractors, and regional geographic chapters.
* **Master Service Agreements:** Negotiating standardized Master Agreements that enforce minimum wage floors, on-shift supervisory ratios, mandatory on-call standby pay, and post-incident rest periods.

---

## 3. Core Governance Pillars of the Guild

* **The Training Trust (JATC):** Administers Related Technical Instruction (RTI) curricula, operates hands-on practical lab facilities, and dispatches vetted apprentices to employer sponsors.
* **The Legal Defense Shield:** Automatically provides indemnification and legal counsel to any licensed member facing corporate retaliation after filing a formal Safety Non-Concurrence, and represents practitioners in malpractice inquiries.
* **The Benefits Trust (Taft-Hartley):** Pools multi-employer contributions to guarantee portable health insurance, disability coverage, parental leave, and retirement accounts that follow workers across employers.
* **The Hiring Hall & Talent Clearinghouse:** Allows employers to source pre-vetted, logbook-audited Journeymen and Apprentices directly, eliminating third-party recruiter markups and enabling flexible staffing during critical migrations or incidents.
