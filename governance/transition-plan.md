# Industry Transition Plan & Grandfathering Bridge

The transition to a licensed trade must not invalidate active practitioners, disrupt ongoing enterprise operations, or penalize self-taught talent. This document outlines the phased bridge model for grandfathering existing professionals, granting Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL), and aligning accredited credentials.

---

## 1. Phased Dual-Track Transition Timeline

* **Years 1–2 (Pilot Cohorts & Criteria Definition):** Launch of regional pilot apprenticeships (Cohort 0) in partnership with community colleges and sponsor enterprises. Formation of the Underwriter Working Group and Board Credential Evaluation Clearinghouse. Existing practitioners register historical tenure.
* **Years 3–4 (Actuarial Codification & Tiered Incentives):** Cyber-insurance underwriters publish formal rate credit schedules based on empirical incident-reduction data from pilot cohorts. Enterprises begin earning premium discounts for certified Journeyman staffing ratios.
* **Year 5+ (Institutional Enforcement):** Direct production sign-offs, critical infrastructure baselines, and statutory regulatory filings require verified Journeyman oversight or Master of Record certification.

## 3. Grandfathering Existing Professionals (Legacy Bridge)

To ensure current practitioners are seamlessly integrated without career disruption, a **5-year legacy grandfathering window** is established upon Board formation:

* **Direct Journeyman Grandfathering:** 5+ years of verified full-time cybersecurity experience OR holding a Board-recognized benchmark credential (e.g., CISSP) grants immediate Journeyman standing upon registration.
* **Master Practitioner Grandfathering:** 8+ years of cumulative senior technical/architectural experience accompanied by peer endorsements from two active senior practitioners.
* **Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL) for Adjacent Tech:** System administrators, DevOps/software engineers, and network engineers receive up to **4,000 hours (50%) credit** toward cybersecurity apprenticeship requirements based on documented past employment.
* **Portfolio Review & Inactive Employer Safe Harbor:** If an applicant's past employer is defunct, acquired, or unable to verify historical logs, the Board provides an expedited portfolio review of code repositories, change records, peer affidavits, or security disclosures to award equivalent standing.

---

## 4. Merit-Based Challenge Pathway (Self-Taught Protections)
To guarantee the trade remains accessible to non-traditional candidates:
* **Vendor-Neutral Practical Exams:** Any candidate, regardless of formal academic degree or employment history, can challenge trade levels by passing hands-on, Board-administered practical lab examinations.
* **Open Source & Research Credit:** Documented contributions to open-source security tools, verified vulnerability disclosures (CVEs/bounties), and public research records may be submitted to the Board for operational hour credits toward trade tiers.
