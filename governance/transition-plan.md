# Industry Transition Plan (The Experience Bridge)

A core requirement of the trade standard is seamless integration with existing senior talent without invalidating established careers.

---

## 1. Direct Endorsement Portability (3-Year Initial Window)
* **Journeyman Status:** Automatically granted to practitioners holding verified credentials requiring 5+ years of verified cumulative experience (e.g., CISSP, CISM, GIAC senior certifications) and in good professional standing.
* **Master Status:** Granted to individuals with 10+ years of documented architectural oversight, principal incident handling, or executive security engineering leadership subject to peer reference endorsement.

---

## 2. Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL) for Adjacent Disciplines
Practitioners transitioning from adjacent technical fields enter the apprenticeship at advanced tiers based on verifiable experience:

* **Systems / Network Administration (3+ Years):** Waives Years 1 & 2 infrastructure rotations; immediate entry at **Tier 3 Apprentice**.
* **Software Engineering (3+ Years):** Waives general computing prerequisites; fast-tracks directly into the **Application Security Specialization Track**.
* **Helpdesk / Desktop Support (2+ Years):** Qualifies as direct fulfillment of Pre-Apprenticeship prerequisites.

---

## 3. The Open-Source & Research Exemption
* **Protected Scope:** Licensure applies strictly to **commercial production environments, client tenant access, and critical infrastructure administration**.
* **Non-Commercial Exemption:** Independent security research, bug bounty participation, academic studies, home laboratories, and open-source tool development require zero licensure.
