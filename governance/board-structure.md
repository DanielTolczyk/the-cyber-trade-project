# National Governance Board & Licensure Administration

The National Cybersecurity Trade Board is an independent, self-regulating non-profit entity responsible for maintaining professional trade standards.

---

## 1. Composition of the Board
The governing board is composed of elected and appointed representatives across key stakeholder groups:
* **40% Master & Journeyman Practitioners:** Active, peer-elected security engineers representing defensive, offensive, cloud, and safety-critical domains.
* **20% Academic & Community College Leaders:** Representatives from accredited technical institutions and registered apprenticeship committees.
* **20% Cyber-Insurance Underwriters & Actuaries:** Risk modeling experts representing corporate underwriting consortiums.
* **20% Public Interest & Regulatory Observers:** Legal ethicists, consumer protection advocates, and public infrastructure liaisons.

---

## 2. Primary Mandates
* **Curriculum Maintenance:** Biannual updates to pre-apprenticeship core competencies and specialty endorsements based on real-world threat evolutions.
* **Registry Management:** Operating the national, tamper-evident registry of licensed apprentices, Journeymen, and Master Practitioners.
* **Ethics & Malpractice Hearings:** Convening formal investigative panels to evaluate claims of technical negligence, gross misconduct, or wrongful retaliation against practitioners exercising the Right of Refusal.
* **Interstate Reciprocity Administration:** Ensuring uniform recognition of licenses across all participating state compacts.
