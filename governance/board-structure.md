# National Governance Board & Regulatory Administration

The National Cybersecurity Trade Board is an independent, self-regulating regulatory entity responsible for public safety, credential standards, and professional licensing. It operates independently from both individual corporate employers and the Cybersecurity Craft Guild.

---

## 1. Multilateral Board Composition
To prevent regulatory capture, anti-competitive closed shops, or corporate dilution of standards, the Board maintains a strictly balanced, multi-stakeholder composition:

* **25% Craft Guild & Labor Representatives:** Peer-elected Journeymen and Master Practitioners representing organized trade members and apprenticeship training trusts (JATCs).
* **25% Enterprise & Independent Industry Employers:** CISO council delegates, commercial engineering leaders, and open-shop enterprise representatives.
* **20% Cyber-Insurance Underwriters & Actuaries:** Risk modeling experts representing corporate underwriting consortiums and reinsurers.
* **15% Academic & Vocational Leaders:** Curriculum deans from accredited community colleges, technical universities, and registered apprenticeship sponsors.
* **15% Public Interest, Legal & Safety Observers:** Consumer privacy advocates, legal ethicists, and critical infrastructure safety liaisons.

---

## 2. Separation of Powers & Non-Discrimination
* **Universal Licensure Standard:** The Board evaluates candidates purely on documented logbook hours, competency assessments, and ethics reviews. Licensure is open to all qualified individuals regardless of Guild affiliation.
* **Independent Examination:** The Board owns and audits the practical milestone challenge exams, ensuring curricula remain neutral, objective, and tied to open standards (e.g., NIST NICE) rather than proprietary commercial interests.
* **Apprenticeship Program Accreditation:** The Board audits and certifies training curricula delivered by both Guild-run JATC facilities and independent enterprise programs.

---

## 3. Core Administrative Mandates

### 1. Licensure Registry & Interstate Compact
* Operating the national, tamper-evident registry of licensed apprentices, Journeymen, and Master Practitioners.
* Administering the Interstate Reciprocity Compact to enable seamless remote work and cross-border commercial practice.

### 2. Malpractice Inquiries & License Sanctions
* Conducting formal evidentiary hearings regarding allegations of gross negligence, willful bypass of security controls, audit falsification, or malicious actions.
* Issuing binding professional sanctions: formal reprimand, mandatory retraining, license suspension, or permanent license revocation.

### 3. Refusal Dispute Adjudication
* Serving as the objective appellate body when an employer disputes a licensed practitioner's formal **Notice of Safety Non-Concurrence**.
* Evaluating whether an engineer's refusal was justified under recognized safety, architectural, and regulatory baselines, and issuing formal determination orders.
