# Regional Chapters, District Councils & Federation Architecture

To ensure the trade remains worker-centric and responsive to local economic conditions, the **Cybersecurity Craft Guild** operates as a decentralized, federated association composed of **Local Chapters ("The Locals")**, **District Councils**, and the **National Delegate Convention**.

## 2. Chartering a Local Chapter ("The Local")

A Local Chapter represents the frontline democratic unit of the trade in a defined metropolitan or regional jurisdiction:

* **Charter Threshold:** A new Local Chapter charter may be petitioned upon organizing **50 active Licensed Journeymen and Master Practitioners** within a defined geographic radius (e.g., Greater Chicago, Washington DC Metro, Pacific Northwest).
* **Local Naming & Numbering:** Local Chapters receive historical charter numbers upon formal recognition by the National Executive Council (e.g., *Cyber Trade Local 101*).
* **Local Officers (Democratically Elected):**
  * **Business Manager / President:** Manages field operations, contract enforcement, and clearinghouse dispatch.
  * **JATC Training Director:** Manages local community college relationships, lab range facilities, and apprentice evaluations.
  * **Treasurer & Recording Secretary:** Audits local dues accounts and records monthly meeting proceedings.
  * **Executive Board (3 to 5 Members):** Elected rank-and-file practitioners providing oversight.
* **Local Democratic Guardrails:**
  * **Direct Secret Ballot:** Local officers are elected directly by the local membership for 3-year terms (max 2 consecutive terms).
  * **Active Practice Mandate:** Local officers must maintain an active Journeyman or Master license and cannot be more than 12 months removed from active practice.
  * **Officer Salary Cap:** Local officer compensation is legally capped at a maximum of 1.25x the local Master of Record prevailing wage floor.
  * **15% Member Recall:** A petition signed by 15% of the local active membership forces an immediate special recall election.

## 3. Geographic Regional District Hubs & Local Chapters

To maintain geographic labor standards, the Craft Guild establishes five permanent Regional District Hubs. Each Local Chapter publishes an official **Metropolitan Statistical Area (MSA) Zone Schedule** mapping regional counties to Zone 1 (Metro Core), Zone 2 (Secondary Metro), or Zone 3 (Rural Baseline) based on federal BLS and HUD Fair Market Rent data:

* **District 1 (Local 101 - Mid-Atlantic & Northeast Hub):** DC, MD, VA, DE, PA, NY, NJ, CT, MA, RI, VT, NH, ME (Zone 1 Metro Core: $85.00/hr RJPB Baseline; Rural Floor: $62.00/hr).
* **District 2 (Local 204 - Midwest & Great Lakes Industrial Hub):** IL, IN, OH, MI, WI, MN, IA, MO (Zone 1 Metro Core: $78.00/hr; Zone 2 Standard Metro: $72.00/hr RJPB Baseline; Rural Floor: $62.00/hr).
* **District 3 (Local 302 - Pacific Northwest & West Coast Hub):** WA, OR, CA, NV, ID, AK, HI (Zone 1 Metro Core: $92.00/hr RJPB Baseline; Zone 2 Secondary Metro: $78.00/hr; Rural Floor: $62.00/hr).
* **District 4 (Local 405 - South & Gulf Coast Energy Hub):** TX, LA, MS, AL, GA, FL, TN, NC, SC (Zone 1 Metro Core: $82.00/hr; Zone 2 Standard Metro: $78.00/hr RJPB Baseline; Rural Floor: $62.00/hr).
* **District 5 (Local 508 - Mountain West & Plains Hub):** CO, UT, AZ, NM, WY, MT, ND, SD, NE, KS (Zone 1 Metro Core: $74.00/hr; Zone 3 General Baseline Floor: $62.00/hr).

## 4. Local Autonomy & Regional Authority

Local Chapters maintain substantial autonomy over matters directly impacting their local membership:

### A. Local Cost-of-Living (COLI) Adjustments
While the National Convention sets the mandatory **Regional Journeyman Prevailing Benchmark (RJPB) Floor**, Local Chapters hold exclusive authority to negotiate higher prevailing local wage scales to match regional housing and living costs (e.g., Local 101 New York setting a +35% COLI local differential above national base).

### B. Local JATC Community College Partnerships
Each Local Chapter's JATC standing committee directly partners with regional community colleges, technical institutes, and municipal workforce boards to establish approved classroom (RTI) campuses, ensuring apprentices learn in local facilities without remote dislocation.

### C. Monthly Member Meetings
Local Chapters conduct mandatory monthly member meetings where rank-and-file engineers, practitioners, and apprentices gather in person to:
* Review local grievance reports and safety non-concurrence filings.
* Share non-attributable defensive threat intelligence and engineering tradecraft.
* Vote on local trust allocations, charitable community cyber clinics, and local policy initiatives.

## 4. National Convention Delegate Apportionment

To ensure large urban locals cannot disenfranchise smaller rural or regional chapters:

* **Proportional Delegate Representation:** Every Local Chapter elects delegates to the Biennial National Convention based on verified active membership:
  * 1 Delegate per 50 active members up to 500 members.
  * 1 Additional Delegate per 150 members thereafter.
* **Constitutional Amendments:** Any change to the National Guild Charter, foundational wage formulas, or member dues schedules requires a **two-thirds (2/3) supermajority vote** of seated convention delegates.
