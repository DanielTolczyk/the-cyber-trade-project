# Board Bylaws, Democratic Elections & Removal Protocols

This specification establishes the constitutional bylaws, democratic electoral procedures, term limits, and member recall protocols for the **National Cybersecurity Trade Board (NCTB)**.

---

## 1. The Interim Founding Steering Committee (Phase 0 Startup)

To bootstrap the trade framework, incorporate the initial non-profit legal entities, and register the National Guidelines for Apprenticeship Standards (NGAS) with the U.S. Department of Labor, the trade is initially administered by an **Interim Founding Steering Committee**:

* **Composition:** 5 to 7 provisional organizing members, including the Founding Organizing Chair, early employer sponsors, vocational educators, and legal counsel.
* **Scope of Authority:** Strictly limited to incorporating the 501(c)(6) non-profit entities, establishing initial practical examination rubrics, filing registered apprenticeship standards under 29 CFR Part 29, and organizing the First National Constitutional Convention.
* **Mandatory 24-Month Sunset Clause:** The Interim Steering Committee automatically dissolves upon reaching **500 active Licensed Journeymen** or **24 months from incorporation**, whichever occurs first. At dissolution, all interim seats are vacated and transferred to democratically elected trustees.
* **Founder Licensure Standing:** The founding architect holds standing as a Licensed Journeyman under objective credential evaluation rubrics. The founder holds no permanent, lifetime, or hereditary governing seats. To serve on the permanent Board, the founder must stand for democratic election alongside all other eligible candidates.

## 3. Democratic Election Procedures & Anti-Coercion Safeguards

### A. Voter Eligibility & Direct Tier Representation
* **Direct Journeyman Voice:** Licensed Journeymen directly elect the Active Journeyman Board Trustee to guarantee frontline engineering realities are represented in standard-setting.
* **Master Practitioner Representation:** Master Practitioners directly elect the two Active Master Practitioner Trustees.
* **Apprentice Franchise:** Registered Apprentices hold exclusive franchise to elect the non-voting **Apprentice Advocate Delegate**, who holds statutory standing to present ratio violation complaints, training safety concerns, and curriculum petitions before the Board.

### B. Anti-Coercion, Supervisory Non-Interference & Safe Harbor Protections
To protect junior practitioners from workplace intimidation, supervisory pressure, and power imbalances:

* **Strict Prohibition of Quid-Pro-Quo & Coercion:** It is a Class-A professional malpractice offense for any supervising Master, Master of Record, employer, or Guild officer to condition an apprentice's wage tier advancement, logbook hour sign-offs, job security, or performance evaluations on how they vote in civic, union, or Board elections.
* **Supervisory Non-Interference Mandate:** Supervisors are strictly banned from soliciting, demanding disclosure of, interrogating, or attempting to influence the vote of any subordinate Journeyman or Apprentice. Coercive campaigning during mandatory on-shift briefings or classroom instruction is prohibited.
* **Immediate Disciplinary Revocation:** Any supervisor who attempts to coerce, intimidate, or retaliate against a subordinate's electoral franchise faces **immediate, mandatory suspension of supervisory authority for a minimum of 24 months** and formal Board disciplinary prosecution.
* **Trainee Career Safe Harbor:** Apprentices and Journeymen may submit confidential reports of supervisory coercion directly to the **Apprentice Advocate Delegate** or the **Guild Legal Defense Shield**. Upon filing, the trainee's accredited runtime hours and wage progression schedule are automatically protected under statutory safe harbor, preventing a hostile supervisor from stalling their career advancement.
* **Cryptographic Secret Ballot:** All balloting is conducted via an auditable, end-to-end verifiable digital ballot system (with paper backup for in-person conventions) that cryptographically decouples voter identity from ballot selections.

### C. Staggered Terms & Term Limits
* **Staggered Rotation:** Board elections occur annually, rotating one-third (1/3) of the voting seats each year to guarantee institutional stability while ensuring regular democratic accountability.
* **Term Limits:** Board members may serve a maximum of **two consecutive 3-year terms (6 years total)**. Following two terms, an individual must observe a mandatory **3-year cooling-off period** before becoming eligible for re-nomination or election.

### D. Ranked-Choice Voting (Instant-Runoff) Protocol
To eliminate vote-splitting, prevent polarizing factional takeovers, and ensure all elected trustees command true majority consensus support:
* **Instant-Runoff Balloting:** In any Board trustee or Guild officer election with three or more candidates, voters rank candidates in order of preference (1st choice, 2nd choice, 3rd choice).
* **Majority Threshold Tabulation:** If no candidate secures an outright majority (50% + 1) of first-choice votes, the candidate with the fewest votes is eliminated, and their ballots are transferred to each voter's next-ranked choice until a candidate achieves a true majority.
* **Single Transferable Vote for Multi-Seat Elections:** When electing multiple trustees concurrently (e.g., electing two Master Practitioners), tabulation follows the Single Transferable Vote (STV) proportional method.

## 4. Conflict of Interest & Commercial Independence

To eliminate commercial vendor capture and pay-to-play credential cartels:

* **Mandatory Financial Disclosures:** All Board candidates and sitting trustees must file annual public financial disclosures enumerating commercial software sponsorships, equity holdings, and consulting retainers.
* **Vendor Exclusion Rule:** No individual currently employed by or holding significant financial equity in a commercial software vendor, proprietary tool developer, or commercial training publisher may occupy the Active Master Practitioner or Public Safety seats.
* **Recusal Protocol:** Board members must formally recuse themselves from deliberations or votes involving entities with which they have had a direct financial or employment relationship within the preceding 24 months.

---

## 5. Member Recall, Disciplinary Suspension & Expulsion

Power in the trade originates from the licensed workforce. Practitioners maintain direct mechanisms to remove corrupt, negligent, or captured officers:

### A. Member-Initiated Recall Petitions
* **Petition Threshold:** A formal recall election for any elected Board trustee is triggered upon submission of a petition signed by **15% of the active licensed membership** in good standing.
* **Recall Ballot:** Upon verification of the petition signatures by an independent election arbiter, a special recall election must be conducted within 45 days. A simple majority vote (50% + 1) of voting members vacates the seat immediately.

### B. Removal for Cause & Malpractice
* **Immediate Suspension:** Any Board member indicted for professional fraud, criminal negligence, accepting commercial kickbacks, or violating Form FORM-001 refusal protections is automatically suspended from all governing duties pending formal review.
* **Board Expulsion Supermajority:** The Board may permanently expel a sitting trustee for gross ethical violations upon a **two-thirds (2/3) supermajority vote** of the full voting quorum following an open evidentiary hearing.

---

## 6. Vacancy Succession

In the event of a death, resignation, recall, or expulsion of an elected trustee:

* **Provisional Appointment:** The remaining Board members may appoint a provisional trustee from the same constituency to serve until the next scheduled annual election.
* **Special Election Rule:** If more than 18 months remain on the vacated term, a special election must be conducted within 60 days to elect a successor for the remainder of the term.



---

## 7. Parliamentary Authority & Open Meetings Transparency

* **Robert's Rules of Order:** Board proceedings follow **Robert's Rules of Order Newly Revised (RONR)** as the parliamentary authority for meeting motions, points of order, and debate decorum.
* **Open Meeting Transparency:** General hearings, examination rubric debates, and rule revisions are live-streamed with public agendas published 30 calendar days in advance.
* **Public Record of Votes:** Every roll-call vote, along with official transcripts and written dissenting opinions from advisory delegates, is published to the public Board Registry within 5 business days.

---

## 8. Statutory Fiduciary Oath to Public Safety

Prior to seating, every elected and appointed Board Trustee must execute the following binding statutory oath:

> *"I do solemnly swear that as a Trustee of the National Cybersecurity Trade Board, my primary legal and ethical fiduciary duty is the protection of public safety, system integrity, and the defense of critical digital infrastructure. I shall execute my duties free from commercial vendor influence, partisan labor bias, or corporate interest, upholding the Trade Canons and defending the ethical safe harbor of the licensed workforce."*
