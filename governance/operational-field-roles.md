## 1. The Frontline Trade Job Steward ("Shop Steward")

* **Role Definition:** The designated frontline Guild representative embedded directly on an active engineering team, SOC shift, or consulting deployment at a participating employer.
* **Appointment & Eligibility:** Appointed by the Local Chapter Business Manager from among active Licensed Journeymen on the employer's regular staff.
* **Core Responsibilities:**
  * **Supervisory Ratio Enforcement:** Monitors active shifts to verify that the mandatory 2:1 on-shift Journeyman-to-Apprentice ratio is strictly maintained across live triage queues and high-privilege operations.
  * **Informal Grievance Triage:** Intervenes in initial logbook verification friction, uncredited on-call standby stipends, or supervisory disagreements before formal escalation.
  * **Interim Safety & Non-Concurrence Support:** Assists apprentices and Journeymen in authoring and timestamping [Operational Exception Flags (Form FORM-003)](../templates/FORM-003_operational-exception-flag.md).
  * **Worker Algorithmic Defense:** Audits workplace tooling to ensure employers adhere to anti-surveillance protections and do not deploy unauthorized bossware, keystroke quotas, or automated termination algorithms.
* **Supervisory Independence:** Shop Stewards are protected by statutory anti-retaliation shields and cannot be disciplined, transferred, or demoted for fulfilling representational duties in good faith.

## 2. The JATC Regional Training Director

* **Role Definition:** The chief administrative officer of the regional Joint Apprenticeship and Training Committee (JATC) trust.
* **Appointment & Governance:** Appointed by bipartisan consensus of the Joint Training Trust Board (equal labor and employer trustees).
* **Core Responsibilities:**
  * **Academic Curriculum Coordination:** Oversees Related Technical Instruction (RTI) course sequences (144+ hours/year) in direct partnership with accredited public community college faculties.
  * **Cyber Range Infrastructure:** Manages regional air-gapped lab hardware, simulation environments, and defensive range exercises funded via employer hourly trust remittances ($1.00/hr).
  * **Logbook Telemetry Auditing:** Conducts quarterly apprentice logbook audits, verifies domain runtime distribution, and certifies milestone wage step elevations (Tiers 1 through 4).
  * **Bipartisan Grievance Hearings:** Convenes formal JATC grievance panels for wage withholding disputes, supervisory friction, or apprentice transfer petitions.



---

## 3. The Board Practical Challenge Examiner

* **Role Definition:** A certified Master Practitioner appointed and accredited by the National Cybersecurity Trade Board to proctor and evaluate objective hands-on licensure challenge exams.
* **Appointment & Accreditation:** Vetted and certified by the Board Examination Committee under psychometric evaluation standards.
* **Core Responsibilities:**
  * **Proctored Challenge Administration:** Proctor live, air-gapped practical examinations evaluating raw CLI navigation, packet analysis, script authoring, and manual threat containment without internet access or generative AI copilots.
  * **Standardized Psychometric Scoring:** Evaluates candidate technical artifacts strictly against objective Board scoring rubrics, eliminating subjective recruiter screening bias.
  * **Strict Conflict-of-Interest Firewall:** Examiners are legally barred from evaluating candidates from their own employer, former direct subordinates, mentees, or relatives.

## 4. The Talent Clearinghouse Dispatch Officer

* **Role Definition:** The neutral administrative officer at the Local or National Guild responsible for operational staffing allocation and apprentice placement.
* **Appointment:** Appointed by the Local Chapter Executive Board.
* **Core Responsibilities:**
  * **Out-of-Work Queue Management (FIFO Three-Book Standard):** Maintains the objective talent referral queue sorted strictly by First-In, First-Out (FIFO) chronological seniority of availability:
    * **Priority Safe Harbor (Top Priority):** Displaced apprentices resulting from employer insolvency, and whistleblowers transferred following substantiated Form FORM-001 technical refusals, guaranteeing zero training hour disruption.
    * **Book 1 (Resident Local Members):** Fully licensed Journeymen and Registered Apprentices residing within that Local Chapter jurisdiction, dispatched strictly in order of out-of-work registration date (longest searching = #1).
    * **Book 2 (Regional Travelers):** Credentialed practitioners registered in sibling Locals traveling into the District, dispatched when local Book 1 labor in that specialty is fully absorbed.
  * **Modality-Aware Dispatch Matching:**
    * **On-Site & Hybrid Requisitions:** Evaluates resident Book 1 candidates within commuting jurisdiction, validating active security clearances (Secret, TS/SCI) when classified SCIF or defense facilities are specified.
    * **Remote Requisitions:** Evaluates the Multi-District Remote Pool across all Locals, dispatching Candidate #1 under FIFO seniority.
  * **Merit-Based Dispatch:** Dispatches qualified practitioners to participating employers based strictly on verified Universal Logbook credentials and Specialty Endorsements, eliminating third-party recruiter placement fees.
  * **Anti-Blacklisting & Turn-Down Protections:** Employers may not arbitrarily reject dispatched practitioners; rejections require an objective operational non-conformance finding submitted within 48 hours. Practitioners may decline up to two dispatch offers without queue penalty.
  * **Emergency Safety Reassignments:** Executes emergency apprentice transfers following substantiated safety non-concurrence or harassment grievances, ensuring zero loss of accredited hours or wage step progress.
