# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. Does this replace a four-year computer science degree?
No. Academic degrees remain valuable for theoretical research, algorithm design, and computer science academia. However, operational cybersecurity is an applied engineering trade. The trade pipeline ensures practical operational competency without requiring arbitrary degree gatekeeping.

### 2. How does this prevent small businesses from being priced out?
Small and medium businesses (SMBs) utilize **Master of Record** or fractional inspection models—identical to construction general contractors hiring an independent Master Plumber to pull permits and inspect rough-in work without needing a full-time executive payroll.

### 3. Will this slow down modern agile development and CI/CD pipelines?
No. Licensed Master Practitioners certify the automated **CI/CD deployment pipelines, policy-as-code rules, and guardrails**. Once the pipeline is verified, developers deploy continuously without manual bottlenecks.

### 4. How does liability work with cloud providers (AWS, Azure, GCP)?
Liability strictly follows the standard Cloud Shared Responsibility Model. Practitioners are accountable only for the customer-controlled layer (IAM, tenant posture, encryption, egress) and are completely shielded from upstream cloud provider outages or hypervisor-level vulnerabilities.

### 5. How are remote, interstate, and offshore teams handled?
* **Interstate Reciprocity:** Modeled after the Interstate Medical Licensure Compact and NCEES standards, a national compact ensures seamless cross-border employment across all states.
* **Offshore/Contract Delegation:** Global or outsourced teams perform operational triage under the line-of-sight supervision and sign-off authority of a licensed Journeyman or Master of Record.

### 6. What about self-taught engineers and hackers?
The framework supports meritocracy through **Practical Challenge Examinations**. Anyone with demonstrated hands-on technical proficiency can test directly into advanced apprentice or journeyman tiers without arbitrary degree requirements.

### 7. Will this restrict open-source development or vulnerability research?
No. Licensure regulates administrative privilege and architectural sign-offs over **production commercial systems and critical infrastructure**. It does not apply to open-source software authoring, personal lab environments, or responsible vulnerability disclosure.
